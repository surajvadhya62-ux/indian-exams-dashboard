Three ways to quiet the exam card

Same two exams, same dark/amber tokens as the live dashboard (--ink, --panel, --amber-bright), three different answers to: how many accent colors and how many buttons does one card actually need?

A

Amber-only

One accent color for the whole product — amber. Domain becomes a small dot, not a stripe or a chip. One primary action; the other two demote to icon-only ghosts.
