## 1. Administrative Framework & Conducting Body

The **Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB)** was incorporated vide Government of NCT of Delhi Notification No. `F.27/7/97-S.III` dated 04-10-1997 issued by the Lieutenant Governor of Delhi. It serves as the apex centralized subordinate cadre recruitment agency for Group 'B' (Non-Gazetted) and Group 'C' posts across all departments of the Government of National Capital Territory of Delhi (GNCTD), Municipal Corporation of Delhi (MCD), New Delhi Municipal Council (NDMC), Delhi Transco Limited (DTL), Delhi Jal Board (DJB), and autonomous state institutions.

- **Headquarters:** DSSSB, FC-18, Institutional Area, Karkardooma, Delhi – 110092
- **Official Domain:** `https://dsssb.delhi.gov.in`
- **Application Portal (OARS):** `https://dsssbonline.nic.in`
- **Results Hub:** `https://dsssb.delhi.gov.in/universal-tab/`
- **Notifications Hub:** `https://dsssb.delhi.gov.in/circulars-orders/`

### Statutory Instruments & Governing Rules:
1. **Pay Commission Jurisdiction:** Unlike general state subordinate boards, all departments under GNCTD and autonomous bodies under Delhi administration operate directly under the **Central Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 2016 (7th Central Pay Commission)**. All posts are placed into the 7th CPC Central Pay Matrix.
2. **Delhi Metro Allowance Class:** Delhi is classified as a **Class 'X'** (higher TPTA metro) city:
   - **House Rent Allowance (HRA):** 30% of Basic Pay (when Central DA crosses 50%, as per Ministry of Finance OM No. 2/5/2017-E.II(B)).
   - **Transport Allowance (TA):** Level 3 through Level 8 receive ₹3,600 + DA (at 58% = ₹5,688 per month).
   - **Dearness Allowance (DA):** Fixed project benchmark of **58%** as of **2025-07-01**.
3. **Examination Schemes:**
   - **Resolution No. P&P/2017/17:** Prescribes standardized examination architectures: One-Tier General (`I-T-G`), One-Tier Technical / Teaching (`I-T-T`), Two-Tier General (`II-T-G`), and Two-Tier Technical (`II-T-T`).
   - Negative marking is standardized at **0.25 marks** per incorrect MCQ response across all tiers.
   - Minimum qualifying criteria: 40% for General / EWS, 35% for OBC (Delhi only), 30% for SC/ST/PwBD.

### Cadre, Selection Scheme & Pay Details
- **Cadre Identity:** Delhi Administration Subordinate Service (DASS) Grade-II represents the premier administrative feeder cadre for the GNCTD secretariat and executive field offices. Appointees are designated as Head Clerk, Assistant Section Officer (ASO), Inspector (Food & Supplies), or Sub-Registrar.
- **Career Ladder:**
  1. DASS Grade-II / Head Clerk / ASO — Level 7 (₹44,900 – ₹1,42,400)
  2. DASS Grade-I / Section Officer / Superintendent — Level 8 (₹47,600 – ₹1,51,100) (approx 5–8 yrs)
  3. DANICS (Junior Time Scale) / Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) / Assistant Commissioner — Level 10 (₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500) (approx 10–16 yrs, 50% quota for DASS Grade-I)
  4. DANICS (Senior Time Scale) / Additional District Magistrate (ADM) / Deputy Commissioner — Level 11 (approx 16–22 yrs)
  5. DANICS Selection Grade / Special Secretary / Director — Level 12 / Level 13 (approx 22–28 yrs, induction into IAS)
- **Selection Architecture:**
  - **Tier-I (Preliminary Screening CBT — General):** 200 MCQs, 200 marks, 2 hours (General Awareness 40, General Intelligence 40, Arithmetical & Numerical Ability 40, Hindi 40, English 40).
  - **Tier-II (Mains Selection CBT & Descriptive):** 275 marks total (3 hours):
    - Part 1 (MCQ - 200 marks): Post-specific administrative law, Indian Polity, Constitution, Governance, and Reasoning.
    - Part 2 (Descriptive - 75 marks): Essay (50 marks) and Letter / Report writing (25 marks) in English.
- **Financial Package:**
  - Pay Level: Level 7 (Entry Basic ₹44,900).
  - 58% DA = ₹26,042; 30% HRA = ₹13,470; TA = ₹5,688 (₹3,600 + DA).
  - Gross Range Estimate: ₹86,000 – ₹94,000.
  - In-Hand Range Estimate: ₹76,000 – ₹84,000 (after 10% NPS deduction ₹7,094, DGEHS, and income tax).
- **Competition Benchmarks:**
  - **2026 Cycle (Advt No. 01/2026):** 617 vacancies (Services Dept revised to 606 vide Corrigendum dt 26-05-2026 + DPCC 8 + DUSIB 3).
  - **2009 Cycle (Post Code 90/09):** 261 vacancies (Result Notice No. 473 dt 11-09-2015: 88,143 appeared in Tier-I; 1,940 shortlisted for Tier-II).
