## 1. Summary of Unit Execution

Unit 99 covers the two premier subordinate, executive, clerical, and field staff competitive recruitment examinations conducted across the Union Territory of Ladakh:
1. `ladakh-ssrb-matric`: Ladakh Subordinate Services Recruitment Board (SSRB) Matric Level Exam — **Tier B (Job)**
2. `ladakh-ssrb-graduate`: Ladakh Administration SSRB Graduate Level Executive & Clerical Exam — **Tier B (Job)**

Primary research and statutory verification were conducted against the official recruitment portals of the Ladakh Subordinate Services Recruitment Board (`https://www.lahdssrb.in`) and the Administration of Union Territory of Ladakh (`https://ladakh.gov.in` and `https://ladakh.gov.in/ssc-corner/`). Every populated data point has been audited against statutory recruitment advertisements, Ladakh Autonomous Hill Development Council service rules, and official provisional select lists.

### Statutory Authorities & Governance Framework:
- **Ladakh Subordinate Services Recruitment Board (LAHD-SSRB / LAHDC Leh SSRB / Kargil DSSRB)**:
  - Formed under the **Ladakh Autonomous Hill Development Council Act, 1997** (Act No. XXXII of 1997) to conduct open competitive examinations for district cadre subordinate, technical, and executive posts across Leh and Kargil districts.
  - In tandem with district-level recruitment, the **Staff Selection Commission (SSC)** conducts recruitment under the **SSC Selection Posts Ladakh** framework for Union Territory and divisional cadre posts pursuant to the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019.
  - All posts mandate possession of a valid **Resident Certificate of Union Territory of Ladakh**, restricting recruitment strictly to domiciled local youth.

### Remuneration & Pay Scale Standards:
- All civilian positions in the Union Territory of Ladakh adhere strictly to the **7th Central Pay Commission (CPC) Pay Matrix**:
  - **Matric Level**: Entry at Level SL-1 (PB-1S ₹4,440–₹7,440, GP ₹1,300, Entry basic pay ₹14,800) for Orderlies, Peons, Attendants, Chowkidars, and Safaiwalas; and Level 2 (PB-1 ₹5,200–₹20,200, GP ₹1,900, Entry basic pay ₹19,900) for Drivers Grade-II, Wildlife Guards, and Forest Guards.
  - **Graduate Level**: Entry at Level 4 (PB-1 ₹5,200–₹20,200, GP ₹2,400, Entry basic pay ₹25,500) for Junior Assistants, Patwaris, and Wasil Baqi Nawis; Level 5 (₹29,200) for Accounts Assistants; and Level 6E (PB-2 ₹9,300–₹34,800, GP ₹4,280, Entry basic pay ₹35,900) for Naib Tehsildars.
- **Dearness Allowance**: Set at the mandatory canonical constant of **58%** as of `2025-07-01` (`da_percent_as_of_review: 58`, `da_as_of: "2025-07-01"`).
- **Special UT Allowances**: In addition to standard 10% Class Z HRA and Transport Allowance (₹900 or ₹1,800 + 58% DA), employees in Ladakh are entitled to Special Compensatory (Remote Locality) Allowance (SCRLA) / Special Duty Allowance (SDA), High Altitude Allowance, and winter heating allowances.

### Examination Scheme & Pattern:
- **Stage 1 (Written Examination)**:
  - Objective Multiple Choice Test (OMR under LAHD-SSRB; CBT under SSC Selection Posts).
  - 100 questions across 4 core domains (25 questions each):
    1. General Intelligence & Reasoning (Verbal & Non-Verbal)
    2. General Awareness (National & Ladakh UT regional awareness, science, polity, history)
    3. Quantitative Aptitude (Basic arithmetic for Matric; arithmetic, algebra, geometry, trigonometry for Graduate)
    4. English Language & Comprehension
  - LAHD-SSRB format: 100 marks (1 mark/Q, 90 mins duration, -0.25 penalty per wrong answer).
  - SSC format: 200 marks (2 marks/Q, 60 mins duration, -0.50 penalty per wrong answer).
- **Stage 2 (Skill / Trade / Physical Endurance Test — Qualifying Only)**:
  - Driving trade test for Driver posts.
  - Computer typing test at min. 35 wpm on keyboard for Junior Assistants.
  - Shorthand test (65 wpm) and typing test (35 wpm) for Steno-Typists.
  - Physical endurance walk of 25 km in 4 hours for male candidates (14 km in 4 hours for female candidates) for Forest Guard and Wildlife Forester posts.
- **Stage 3**: Document Verification and Medical Examination.

- **Pay Scale**: Level SL-1 (PB-1S ₹14,800 - ₹47,100) and Level 2 (PB-1 ₹19,900 - ₹63,200). Entry basic pay ₹14,800. - **Career Trajectory**: Orderly / Peon (Level SL-1) / Driver / Guard (Level 2) → Jamadar / Senior Orderly / Driver Grade-I (Level 2/3, ₹19,900–₹69,100) → Daftari / Record Keeper / Junior Assistant (Level 4, ₹25,500–₹81,100) → Senior Assistant (Level 5, ₹29,200–₹92,300) → Office Superintendent / Section Officer (Level 7, ₹44,900–₹1,42,400).
