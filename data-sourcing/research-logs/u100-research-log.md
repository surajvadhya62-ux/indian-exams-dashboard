### Constitutional & Administrative Framework:
- **Territorial Administration**:
  - The Union Territory of Lakshadweep is administered directly by the Central Government through an Administrator appointed by the President of India under **Article 239 of the Constitution of India**, functioning under the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), Government of India.
  - All civilian personnel appointed to the Lakshadweep Administration Secretariat, Collectorate, and line Directorates (Services, Education, Health, Police, Revenue, PWD, Electricity, Marine Fisheries) are Central Civil Services (CCS) employees governed by Central Civil Services rules and the **7th Central Pay Commission (7th CPC) Pay Matrix**.
  - Direct recruitment for Group 'C' subordinate ministerial, clerical, technical, and executive posts is conducted centrally through Departmental Selection Boards / Staff Selection Boards appointed by the Administrator, in coordination with the District Employment Exchange, Kavaratti.
  - Native islanders (more than 95% belonging to the Scheduled Tribe / ST category) receive statutory preference / domicile reservation for island cadre postings.

### Remuneration & Pay Scale Standards:
- **Central 7th CPC Pay Matrix**:
  - **Foundational Clerical Cadre**: Lower Division Clerk (LDC) / Junior Assistant / Deputy Nazir is positioned at **Pay Level 2** (PB-1 ₹5,200–₹20,200, Grade Pay ₹1,900; revised basic pay ₹19,900 – ₹63,200 with entry basic pay ₹19,900; statutorily notified in Lakshadweep Gazette Extraordinary Vol. LXII No. 11 dated 18/05/2026, Notification F.No. 2/2018-Estt/DC (3)).
  - **Field & Law Enforcement Cadre**: Police Constable (Executive) is positioned at **Pay Level 3** (entry basic pay ₹21,700; scale ₹21,700 – ₹69,100; verified in Lakshadweep Gazette Extraordinary Vol. LXII No. 38 dated 16/08/2026, Schedule-III).
  - **Upper Clerical Cadre**: Upper Division Clerk (UDC) / Head Clerk is positioned at **Pay Level 4** (entry basic pay ₹25,500; scale ₹25,500 – ₹81,100; Gazette Vol. LXII No. 11, Schedule-I).
- **Dearness Allowance**: Set at the mandatory canonical constant of **58%** as of `2025-07-01` (`da_percent_as_of_review: 58`, `da_as_of: "2025-07-01"`).
- **Special Island Allowances & Compensations**:
  - **Island Special Duty Allowance (ISDA)**: Sanctioned under Ministry of Finance Department of Expenditure orders to compensate for maritime isolation, remote atoll conditions, and severe logistics constraints (ranging from 12.5% to 20% of basic pay depending on the island posting zone).
  - **House Rent Allowance (HRA)**: 9% Class Z rate or government residential quarters in Kavaratti / island stations.
  - **Transport Allowance (TPTA)**: ₹1,350 + 58% DA (₹2,133) or local island conveyance.
  - **Island Leave Travel Concession (Island LTC)**: Includes subsidized passenger ship passage (all-weather ships such as MV Kavaratti, MV Arabian Sea) and subsidized helicopter / passenger flight travel connecting Kochi / Kozhikode to Agatti / Kavaratti per Central Government orders.
  - **Medical Referral Facilities**: Central Services (Medical Attendance) rules with full referral reimbursement to mainland empanelled tertiary hospitals in Kochi (Kerala).
  - **Retirement & Welfare**: National Pension System (NPS) with 14% Central Government matching contribution and CGEGIS coverage.

### Examination Scheme & Pattern:
- **Stage 1: Objective Written Competitive Examination (OMR / Computer Based Test)**:
  - 100 MCQs for 100 marks (or 200 questions for 200 marks depending on cadre notification) of 120 minutes (2 hours) duration.
  - Bilingual (English and Malayalam / Hindi, with English section in English only).
  - Negative marking: 0.25 marks deducted per wrong answer.
  - 4 core domains (25 marks each):
    1. General English (Grammar, Vocabulary, Sentence Correction, Comprehension)
    2. General Knowledge, Current Affairs & Lakshadweep Island Awareness
    3. Numerical Aptitude & Elementary Mathematics (Arithmetic, Percentages, Ratios, Averages)
    4. General Intelligence & Reasoning Ability (Verbal & Non-Verbal Reasoning)
- **Stage 2: Skill / Trade / Physical Endurance Test (Qualifying Only)**:
  - For Clerical / Typist Posts: Computer Typing Speed Test (minimum 35 wpm in English [10,500 KDPH] or 30 wpm in Hindi/Malayalam on computer).
  - For Police Constable / Field Posts: Physical Standard & Endurance Test (1600m run in 6 min 30 sec, 100m swimming in 2 min, long jump 13 feet in 3 chances per Gazette Vol. LXII No. 38 Schedule-III).
- **Stage 3**: Document Verification and Medical Examination at Kavaratti General Hospital.
