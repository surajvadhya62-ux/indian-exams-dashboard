## 1. Statutory Authorities & CPSE Corporate Frameworks

Unit `u136` represents the culminating batch of the data sourcing effort, encompassing 14 key examinations across India's premier Maharatna, Navratna, and Miniratna Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs) under respective administrative ministries (Civil Aviation, Power, Petroleum & Natural Gas, Defence, Coal, Mines) as well as the Department of Posts (Ministry of Communications):

### A. Civil Aviation & Airports Infrastructure
1. **Airports Authority of India (AAI — `aai-junior-executive-common`)**:
   - Statutory body created by an Act of Parliament (Airports Authority of India Act, 1994) under the Ministry of Civil Aviation.
   - Official Portal: `https://www.aai.aero`.
   - Cadre: Junior Executive (Common Cadre) — Non-technical executive cadre created to manage airport administration, operations, commercial concessions, and HR.
   - Pay Scale: IDA Grade E-1 (`₹40,000 – ₹1,40,000`), canonical 58% DA constant applied.

### B. Power & Energy Maharatnas / Miniratnas
2. **NTPC Limited (`ntpc-et`)**:
   - Maharatna CPSE under the Ministry of Power, India's largest power generator.
   - Official Portal: `https://careers.ntpc.co.in` / `https://www.ntpc.co.in`.
   - Cadre: Engineering Executive Trainee (EET) via GATE.
   - Pay Scale: Grade E-1 (`₹40,000 – ₹1,40,000`) during training; Grade E-2 (`₹50,000 – ₹1,60,000`) on absorption.

3. **Power Grid Corporation of India Limited (POWERGRID — `pgcil-et`)**:
   - Maharatna CPSE under Ministry of Power; manages national electric transmission grid.
   - Official Portal: `https://www.powergrid.in`.
   - Cadre: Executive Trainee (Electrical/Civil/Electronics/CS) via GATE.
   - Pay Scale: Grade E-1 (`₹40,000 – ₹1,40,000`) during training; Grade E-2 (`₹50,000 – ₹1,60,000`) on absorption.

4. **NHPC Limited (`nhpc-trainee-engineer`)**:
   - Premier hydropower utility under the Ministry of Power.
   - Official Portal: `https://www.nhpcindia.com`.
   - Cadre: Trainee Engineer & Trainee Officer.
   - Pay Scale: Grade E-2 (`₹50,000 – ₹1,60,000`).

5. **SJVN Limited (`sjvn-field-engineer`)**:
   - Joint venture between Govt. of India and Govt. of Himachal Pradesh under Ministry of Power.
   - Official Portal: `https://sjvn.nic.in`.
   - Cadre: Field Engineer & Executive Trainee.
   - Pay Scale: Grade E-2 (`₹50,000 – ₹1,60,000`).

6. **THDC India Limited (`thdc-engineer-trainee`)**:
   - Schedule 'A' CPSE, joint venture of NTPC Ltd and Govt of UP, Ministry of Power.
   - Official Portal: `https://www.thdc.co.in`.
   - Cadre: Engineer Trainee via GATE.
   - Pay Scale: Grade E-2 (`₹50,000 – ₹1,60,000`) during training; Grade E-3 (`₹60,000 – ₹1,80,000`) on absorption.

### C. Hydrocarbons & Oil Maharatnas
7. **Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL — `iocl-apprentice` & `iocl-finance-officer`)**:
   - India's flagship Maharatna national oil company under Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas.
   - Official Portal: `https://iocl.com`.
   - Cadres:
     - `iocl-apprentice`: Statutory apprentice engagement across Refineries, Pipelines, and Marketing Divisions under Apprentices Act, 1961, and transition into non-executive Grade IV (`₹25,000 – ₹1,05,000`).
     - `iocl-finance-officer`: Officer (Finance) in Grade A (`₹50,000 – ₹1,60,000`).

8. **Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited (ONGC — `ongc-finance-officer`)**:
   - Maharatna upstream crude oil and natural gas explorer under Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas.
   - Official Portal: `https://www.ongcindia.com`.
   - Cadre: Finance & Accounts Officer (E-1, `₹60,000 – ₹1,80,000`).

### D. Heavy Engineering, Defence & Mining Maharatnas / Navratnas
9. **Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL — `hal-management-trainee`)**:
   - Premier aerospace and defence CPSE under Department of Defence Production, Ministry of Defence.
   - Official Portal: `https://hal-india.co.in`.
   - Cadre: Management Trainee (Technical / Non-Technical) & Design Trainee in Grade II (`₹40,000 – ₹1,40,000`).

10. **Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (BHEL — `bhel-finance-executive`)**:
    - Maharatna engineering and manufacturing conglomerate under Ministry of Heavy Industries.
    - Official Portal: `https://www.bhel.com` / `https://careers.bhel.in`.
    - Cadre: Executive Trainee (Finance) in Grade E-1 (`₹50,000 – ₹1,60,000`).

11. **Coal India Limited (CIL — `cil-mt`)**:
    - Maharatna coal mining behemoth under Ministry of Coal.
    - Official Portal: `https://www.coalindia.in`.
    - Cadre: Management Trainee across mining, engineering, and corporate disciplines in Grade E-2 (`₹50,000 – ₹1,60,000`).

12. **National Aluminium Company Limited (NALCO — `nalco-mt`)**:
    - Navratna bauxite mining and integrated aluminium CPSE under Ministry of Mines.
    - Official Portal: `https://www.nalcoindia.com`.
    - Cadre: Management Trainee via GATE in Grade E-1 (`₹40,000 – ₹1,40,000`).

### E. Central Communications & Postal Network
13. **Department of Posts (India Post — `india-post-mts-postman`)**:
    - Central Government Department under Ministry of Communications.
    - Official Portal: `https://www.indiapost.gov.in`.
    - Cadres: Postman / Mail Guard (7th CPC Pay Matrix Level 3, `₹21,700 – ₹69,100`) and Multi Tasking Staff (Level 1, `₹18,000 – ₹56,900`).
