## 1. Statutory Authorities & Regulatory Frameworks

Unit `u132` comprises eight premier examinations across national professional bodies, statutory financial and food safety regulators, and central agricultural scientific recruitment boards:

### A. Professional Accounting & Governance Institutes (Parliamentary Charters)
1. **The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI — `ca`)**:
   - Established by the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 (Act No. XXXVIII of 1949).
   - Headquarters: ICAI Bhawan, Indraprastha Marg, New Delhi.
   - Official Portal: `https://www.icai.org`.
   - Governance: New Scheme of Education and Training notified under Regulation 25E of the Chartered Accountants Regulations, 1988 (effective 1 July 2023; examinations commencing May 2024). Three tiers: Foundation (4 papers), Intermediate (2 groups, 6 papers), and Final (2 groups, 6 papers) with mandatory Self-Paced Online Modules (SPOM) and 2-year practical training (Articleship).
   - Exam Type: `entrance` (Career ladder and financial package strictly omitted per schema rules).

2. **The Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI — `cs`)**:
   - Established by the Company Secretaries Act, 1980 (Act No. 56 of 1980) under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.
   - Headquarters: ICSI House, 22, Institutional Area, Lodi Road, New Delhi.
   - Official Portal: `https://www.icsi.edu`.
   - Governance: ICSI Syllabus 2022 framework consisting of CSEET (Company Secretary Executive Entrance Test, 4 parts, 200 marks, remote proctored CBT), Executive Programme (2 groups, 7 papers), and Professional Programme (2 groups, 7 papers including electives) with mandatory 21-month corporate training and CLDP.
   - Exam Type: `entrance` (Career ladder and financial package strictly omitted per schema rules).

3. **The Institute of Cost Accountants of India (ICMAI — `cma`)**:
   - Established by the Cost and Works Accountants Act, 1959 (Act No. 23 of 1959) under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.
   - Headquarters: CMA Bhawan, 12, Sudder Street, Kolkata.
   - Official Portal: `https://icmai.in`.
   - Governance: CMA Syllabus 2022 comprising Foundation (4 papers, 400 marks, objective MCQ), Intermediate (2 groups, 8 papers), and Final (2 groups, 8 papers) with SAP Finance Power User training and mandatory 15-month practical training.
   - Exam Type: `entrance` (Career ladder and financial package strictly omitted per schema rules).

### B. Unified Offshore Financial Regulator
4. **International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA — `ifsca-grade-a`)**:
   - Statutory unified regulator established under Section 4(1) of the International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019 (Act No. 50 of 2019).
   - Headquarters: PRAGYA, GIFT City, Gandhinagar – 382355, Gujarat.
   - Official Portal: `https://www.ifsca.gov.in`.
   - Cadre: Officer Grade 'A' (Assistant Manager) recruited through a three-stage national competitive process (Phase I Online Screening, Phase II Online Examination with 1/3rd descriptive English and 2/3rd domain regulations weightage, and Phase III Interview).
   - Pay Scale: Special regulatory Grade A officer pay scale (`₹44,500 – 2,500(4) – 54,500 – 2,850(7) – 74,450 – EB – 2,850(4) – 85,850 – 3,300(1) – 89,150`), matching SEBI/RBI regulatory cadres. Canonical 58% DA constant applied.

### C. National Food Safety & Standards Regulator
5. **Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI — `fssai-cfso` & `fssai-technical-officer`)**:
   - Apex statutory food regulator established under Section 4 of the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 under the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare.
   - Headquarters: FDA Bhawan, Kotla Road, New Delhi – 110002.
   - Official Portal: `https://www.fssai.gov.in`.
   - Cadres Processed:
     - `fssai-cfso`: Central Food Safety Officer (Group B, 7th CPC Level 7, entry basic pay ₹44,900), responsible for statutory food inspection, sampling, licensing, and import clearance.
     - `fssai-technical-officer`: Technical Officer (Group B, 7th CPC Level 7, entry basic pay ₹44,900), responsible for science & standards formulation, lab testing oversight, and risk assessment.
   - Exam Architecture: Two-stage Computer Based Test (CBT Stage 1 screening 120 Qs / 480 marks; CBT Stage 2 written test 80 Qs / 320 marks; marking scheme +4 correct, -1 wrong) followed by Document Verification.
   - Pay Scale: 7th CPC Level 7 (`₹44,900 – ₹1,42,400`), canonical 58% DA constant applied.

### D. Central Agricultural Research & Scientists Recruitment Boards
6. **Agricultural Scientists Recruitment Board (ASRB — `asrb-icar-administrative-officer`)**:
   - Independent recruitment body under Department of Agricultural Research and Education (DARE), Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare.
   - Headquarters: KAB-I, Pusa, New Delhi – 110012.
   - Official Portal: `https://www.asrb.org.in`.
   - Cadre: Administrative Officer (AO) and Finance & Accounts Officer (FAO) across 64+ ICAR research institutes. Group 'A' Gazetted, 7th CPC Level 10 (entry basic pay ₹56,100; `₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500`).
   - Exam Architecture: Three-tier process: Tier I Objective CBT (200 marks, 4 sections, 1/3rd negative marking), Tier II Descriptive Mains (4 papers, 150 marks each = 600 marks), and Tier III Interview (100 marks); final merit out of 700 marks.

7. **ICAR - Indian Agricultural Research Institute (ICAR-IARI — `icar-iari-technician`)**:
   - Premier agricultural research institute of ICAR (Deemed University under UGC Act, 1956).
   - Headquarters: Pusa Campus, New Delhi – 110012.
   - Official Portal: `https://www.iari.res.in` (and ICAR headquarters `https://icar.org.in`).
   - Cadre: Technician (T-1), Category I technical service cadre across all ICAR institutes. Group 'C' Non-Gazetted Technical, 7th CPC Level 3 (entry basic pay ₹21,700; `₹21,700 – ₹69,100`).
   - Promotion: Governed by the ICAR Technical Service Rules (TSR) Assessment Scheme (merit-based time-bound 5-year review independent of vacancy constraints).
   - Exam Architecture: 100 Objective MCQs, 90 minutes, 4 sections (GK, Maths, Science, Social Science, 25 marks each), 0.25 negative marking.
