## 1. Statutory Authorities & Defence Recruitment Framework

The 11 examinations in Unit `u128` cover defence research, armed forces enlistment under the Agnipath scheme, maritime security cadres, civilian defence technical establishments, air force admissions, military medical education, and premier counter-terrorism investigation:

### A. Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO)
- **Authority**: Ministry of Defence, Government of India.
- **Cadres & Examinations**:
  - `drdo-ceptam`: Recruits non-gazetted technical personnel under Defence Research Technical Cadre (DRTC). STA-B enters at 7th CPC Level 6 (basic ₹35,400) and Technician-A enters at Level 2 (basic ₹19,900). Two-tier CBT structure with domain specialization.
  - `drdo-set` (RAC Scientist 'B'): Recruits Group 'A' Gazetted research scientists under Defence Research & Development Service (DRDS) via GATE scores / descriptive examinations followed by Personal Interview. Level 10 (basic ₹56,100). Merit-based career progression operates under Flexible Complementing Scheme (FCS).

### B. Armed Forces Agnipath & Commissioned Entries
- **Authority**: Indian Navy & Indian Air Force, Ministry of Defence.
- **Examinations Covered**:
  - `agniveer-navy`: 4-year tenure with customized package (₹30,000 to ₹40,000 per month) + tax-exempt Seva Nidhi package of ~₹11.71 Lakh + non-contributory ₹48 Lakh insurance. Up to 25% absorbed into regular Sailor cadre in 7th CPC Level 3 (basic ₹21,700). Selection via INET CBT, PFT, and INS Chilka medicals.
  - `agniveer-air-force`: 4-year Agniveervayu tenure with matching financial package and Seva Nidhi. Up to 25% absorbed into regular IAF Airmen cadre at Level 3 (basic ₹21,700). Online test across Science / Other Than Science / Both, followed by PFT, Adaptability Tests, and Medicals.
  - `afcat`: Commissioned officer entrance test for Flying, Technical, and Ground Duty branches. Online CBT (100 questions, 300 marks, 120 mins) followed by 5-day Air Force Selection Board (AFSB) testing. Strictly omits `career_ladder` and `financial_package` as an entrance exam.
  - `afmc-mbbs`: Armed Forces Medical College MBBS admission at Pune. Screening based on NEET-UG score + Test of English Language, Comprehension, Logic and Reasoning (ToELR - 40 MCQs, 80 marks) + Psychological Assessment Test (PAT) + Personal Interview (50 marks). Strictly omits `career_ladder` and `financial_package`.

### C. Maritime & Civilian Defence Establishments
- **Indian Coast Guard (`icg-navik-db`)**: Armed force of the Union under Ministry of Defence. Navik (Domestic Branch - Cook & Steward) enters at Level 3 (basic ₹21,700) with promotions through Adhikari ranks up to Level 8.
- **Military Engineer Services (`mes-supervisor-barrack-store`)**: Engineer-in-Chief's Branch, MoD. Supervisor Barrack & Store (SBS) enters as Group 'B' Non-Gazetted in Level 6 (basic ₹35,400) and rises to Director level (Level 12).
- **Indian Navy Civilian Technical Cadre (`indian-navy-chargeman`)**: Directorate of Civilian Personnel. Chargeman enters at Level 6 (basic ₹35,400) via INCET CBT across Reasoning, GA, Quant, and English (100 MCQs, 90 mins).
- **IAF Civilian Group 'C' (`iaf-civilian-group-c`)**: Station and maintenance commands civilian staff. Multi Tasking Staff (MTS) enters at Level 1 (basic ₹18,000) and LDC/Storekeeper enters at Level 2 (basic ₹19,900) via 100-mark written test + qualifying trade/typing test.

### D. Counter-Terrorism Law Enforcement
- **National Investigation Agency (`nia-si-inspector`)**: Premier counter-terror agency under Ministry of Home Affairs. Sub-Inspector enters at Level 6 (basic ₹35,400) via SSC CGL Examination. Features 20% Special Security Allowance (SSA) + 30 days additional annual cash compensation.
