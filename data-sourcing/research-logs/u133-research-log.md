## 1. Statutory Authorities & Regulatory Frameworks

Unit `u133` covers five critical examinations across India's supreme audit institution, national standards body, autonomous forestry scientific council, and national geological survey:

### A. Supreme Audit Institution of India
1. **Comptroller and Auditor General of India (CAG / IA&AD — `cag-sas`)**:
   - Constitutional authority established under Article 148 of the Constitution of India; powers and duties governed by the Comptroller and Auditor General's (Duties, Powers and Conditions of Service) Act, 1971 (Act No. 56 of 1971).
   - Headquarters: Pocket 9, Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Marg, New Delhi – 110124.
   - Official Portal: `https://cag.gov.in`.
   - Governance: Subordinate Audit/Accounts Service (SAS) Examination is the mandatory qualifying examination for confirmation and regular appointment of Direct Recruit Assistant Audit Officers (DR-AAOs) and departmental promotion of serving staff (Auditors, Accountants, Senior Auditors, Senior Accountants, Stenographers, DEOs, and Divisional Accountants) to the Group 'B' Gazetted cadre of Assistant Audit Officer (AAO) / Assistant Accounts Officer (AAO).
   - Cadre & Pay Scale: Group 'B' Gazetted, 7th CPC Level 8 (entry basic pay ₹47,600; `₹47,600 – ₹1,51,100`). Automatic non-functional upgradation to Level 9 (`₹53,100 – ₹1,67,800`) after 4 years of regular service, followed by Senior Audit Officer (Level 10, `₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500`) and induction into the Indian Audit and Accounts Service (IA&AS).
   - Examination Scheme: Conducted biannually in CBT mode (Exam-1 and Exam-2). Two tiers: field office preliminary screening test and headquarters CBT examination covering Group-I (Language Skills descriptive, Government Audit, IT theory/practical, and Financial Rules) and Group-II (Advanced Accounts, GASAB, Performance & Compliance Audit, and Commercial Laws).

### B. National Standards Body of India
2. **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS — `bis-technical-assistant` & `bis-scientist-b`)**:
   - Statutory body established under the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016 (Act No. 11 of 2016) under the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution, Government of India.
   - Headquarters: Manak Bhavan, 9 Bahadur Shah Zafar Marg, New Delhi – 110002.
   - Official Portal: `https://www.bis.gov.in`.
   - Cadres Processed:
     - `bis-technical-assistant`: Technical Assistant (Laboratory) in Pay Level 6 (entry basic pay ₹35,400; `₹35,400 – ₹1,12,400`) and Senior Secretariat Assistant (SSA) in Pay Level 4 (`₹25,500 – ₹81,100`). Technical assistants execute standard compliance testing (mechanical, chemical, microbiological, electrical) across BIS Central and Regional Laboratories for ISI certification and Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS). Exam scheme consists of an online CBT (150 MCQs / 150 marks / 120 mins) followed by a practical laboratory skill test.
     - `bis-scientist-b`: Group 'A' Gazetted / Officer cadre in Pay Level 10 (entry basic pay ₹56,100; `₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500`). Recruited via valid GATE score (85% weightage) in relevant engineering branches (Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, Chemical, Metallurgical, Computer Engineering) followed by Personal Interview (15% weightage). Promotions are governed by the Modified Flexible Complementing Scheme (FCS) enabling merit-based advancement independent of vacancies up to Scientist 'G' (Level 14).

### C. Apex Forestry Research Council
3. **Indian Council of Forestry Research and Education (ICFRE — `icfre-technical-assistant`)**:
   - Autonomous body under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), Government of India.
   - Headquarters: P.O. New Forest, Dehradun – 248006, Uttarakhand.
   - Official Portal: `https://www.icfre.org` / `https://recruitment.icfre.gov.in/`.
   - Constituent Institutes: Forest Research Institute (FRI) Dehradun, Tropical Forest Research Institute (TFRI) Jabalpur, Institute of Forest Genetics and Tree Breeding (IFGTB) Coimbatore, AFRI Jodhpur, HFRI Shimla, RFRI Jorhat, IWST Bengaluru, IFB Hyderabad, and IFP Ranchi.
   - Cadre: Technical Assistant (Field / Lab / Maintenance), Category II technical service cadre. Group 'C' Non-Gazetted Technical, 7th CPC Level 5 (entry basic pay ₹29,200; `₹29,200 – ₹92,300`).
   - Exam Scheme: Written examination (100 MCQs, 100 marks, 120 minutes) covering General Awareness (20), Reasoning (20), General English (20), and Subject Domain (Botany/Forestry/Chemistry/Zoology) (40), with -0.25 negative marking. Promotion governed by ICFRE Technical Service Rules 5-year assessment system.

### D. National Geological Survey & Mineral Exploration Organization
4. **Geological Survey of India (GSI — `gsi-driver-mechanic`)**:
   - Attached office of the Ministry of Mines, Government of India, established in 1851.
   - Central Headquarters (CHQ): 27, Jawaharlal Nehru Road, Kolkata – 700016, with regional headquarters in Lucknow, Jaipur, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Nagpur, and Shillong.
   - Official Portal: `https://www.gsi.gov.in` (and Ministry portal `https://mines.gov.in`).
   - Cadre: Driver (Ordinary Grade) / Mechanical Technical Staff. Group 'C' Non-Gazetted Technical, 7th CPC Level 2 (entry basic pay ₹19,900; `₹19,900 – ₹63,200`). Responsible for operating and maintaining 4x4 heavy field trucks, drilling carriers, and expedition vehicles in remote geological fieldwork and mining camps.
   - Exam Scheme: Regional-level recruitment comprising a Written Examination (100 marks OMR objective test on General Knowledge, Reasoning, Traffic Rules, and Vehicle Mechanism) followed by a mandatory Practical Driving and Mechanical Fault Rectification Trade Test (100 marks qualifying).
