## 1. Statutory Authorities, CPSE Profiles & Regulatory Frameworks

Unit `u134` covers 8 premier competitive examinations conducted by Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs), statutory corporations, and central government departments spanning civil aviation, postal communications, national electric transmission, dedicated rail freight, aerospace defence, defence electronics, and downstream petroleum refining:

### A. Civil Aviation & Air Navigation Services
1. **Airports Authority of India (AAI — `aai-atc`)**:
   - Statutory body created by the Airports Authority of India Act, 1994 (Act No. 55 of 1994) under the Ministry of Civil Aviation, managing 137 airports and sovereign Indian civil airspace.
   - Official Portal: `https://www.aai.aero`.
   - Cadre: Junior Executive (Air Traffic Control) [Group B, E-1 Level].
   - Pay Scale: IDA Pay Scale E-1 (`₹40,000 – 3% – ₹1,40,000`), Entry Basic Pay ₹40,000. Approximate CTC: ₹13 Lakhs per annum. Canonical 58% DA constant applied.
   - Primary Document Verified: Advt. No. 02/2025/CHQ (`Detailed ATC Advertisement 02-2025-CHQ.pdf`, 8 pages opened and read with `pdftotext`).
   - Selection Scheme: Computer Based Test (120 questions, 120 marks, 120 minutes, no negative marking; Part A: General Aptitude 60 marks, Part B: Core Physics & Mathematics 60 marks), followed by Application Verification, Voice Test (clear voice without stammering/lisping + ICAO Level 4 proficiency), Psychoactive Substances Test, Psychological Assessment, and Pre-Employment Medical Examination (ICAO Class 3).

### B. Rural Communications & Postal Services
2. **Department of Posts, Ministry of Communications (`india-post-gds`)**:
   - Central Government department administering one of the largest postal networks in the world.
   - Official Portals: `https://indiapostgdsonline.gov.in`, `https://www.indiapost.gov.in`.
   - Cadre: Gramin Dak Sevaks (Branch Postmaster - BPM, Assistant Branch Postmaster - ABPM, Dak Sevak).
   - Pay Scale: Time Related Continuity Allowance (TRCA) governed by GDS (Conduct and Engagement) Rules and Kamlesh Chandra Committee recommendations. Minimum TRCA Level 1: BPM ₹12,000 (scale ₹12,000 – ₹29,380); ABPM/Dak Sevak ₹10,000 (scale ₹10,000 – ₹24,470). Dearness Allowance on TRCA aligned with Central Government rates (canonical 58% DA constant applied).
   - Primary Document Verified: GDS Model Notification (`Model_Notification.pdf`, 18 pages opened and read with `pdftotext`).
   - Selection Scheme: 100% automated merit list based on Class 10 (Matriculation) examination marks (Mathematics, English, local language compulsory), followed by physical document verification and accommodation inspection for Branch Post Office. No written exam or interview.

### C. Power Transmission Infrastructure
3. **Power Grid Corporation of India Limited (POWERGRID / PGCIL — `pgcil-diploma-trainee`)**:
   - Maharatna CPSE under the Ministry of Power, transmitting approximately 85% of India's inter-regional power.
   - Official Portal: `https://www.powergrid.in`.
   - Cadre: Diploma Trainee (Electrical / Civil / Survey).
   - Pay Scale: S0 level during 1-yr training (Stipend Basic ₹24,000, IDA Scale ₹24,000 – ₹1,08,000). On regular absorption: Junior Engineer Gr-IV at S1 level in IDA scale `₹25,000 – 3% – ₹1,17,500`. Annual CTC: approx ₹12.7 Lakhs. Canonical 58% DA constant applied.
   - Primary Documents Verified: Detailed Advertisement - Non Executive Posts 2026 (Advt CC/01/2026, 16 pages), Notice-10 (Revised count of applications received), and Notice-11 (Count of candidates appeared in CBT).
   - Selection Scheme: Written Test / CBT (170 MCQs, 170 marks, 120 minutes; Part I Technical Knowledge 120 MCQs, Part II Aptitude Test 50 MCQs; -0.25 negative marking; 100% weightage for final selection), followed by Document Verification and Pre-Employment Medical Examination.

### D. Rail Freight Logistics
4. **Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Limited (DFCCIL — `dfccil-executive`)**:
   - Special Purpose Vehicle / CPSE under the Ministry of Railways developing Eastern and Western DFCs.
   - Official Portal: `https://dfccil.com`.
   - Cadre: Executive (Civil, Electrical, Signal & Telecommunications, Operations & Business Development).
   - Pay Scale: IDA Scale E-0 (`₹30,000 – ₹1,20,000`), Entry Basic Pay ₹30,000. Canonical 58% DA constant applied.
   - Primary Documents Verified: Advt. No. 01/DR/2025 (`Advertisement-2024-(09-01-2025-Final)-updated-Link_KFYD.pdf`, 40 pages) and Official Application Count Notice (`Notice-for-Number-of-Application-Received_IPGQ.pdf`).
   - Selection Scheme: Two-stage CBT (1st Stage CBT screening: 100 MCQs, 90 mins, -0.25 negative marking, shortlists 20x vacancies; 2nd Stage CBT merit-forming: 120 MCQs, 120 mins, -0.25 negative marking), followed by CBAT (for OP&BD only), Document Verification, and Medical Examination (Aye-Three / Cee-One standards).

### E. Aerospace & Defence Manufacturing
5. **Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL — `hal-design-trainee`)**:
   - Maharatna defence CPSE under the Ministry of Defence, designer and manufacturer of LCA Tejas, ALH Dhruv, LCH Prachand, and aero-engines.
   - Official Portal: `https://hal-india.co.in`.
   - Cadre: Design Trainee (DT) and Management Trainee (MT).
   - Pay Scale: 52 weeks training with Basic Pay ₹50,000 + VDA + canteen allowance + bachelor accommodation. Absorbed as Engineer / Officer in Grade-II in IDA scale `₹50,000 – ₹1,60,000`. Annual CTC: approx ₹15 - 18 Lakhs. Canonical 58% DA constant applied.
   - Primary Document Verified: Advt. No. HAL/CHRC-TM/RECT-02/2026 (`Detailed_Advertisement_29072026.pdf`, 13 pages opened and read with `pdftotext`).
   - Selection Scheme: All-India Online Selection Test (160 MCQs, 150 minutes, no negative marking; Part I General Awareness 20 MCQs, Part II English & Reasoning 40 MCQs, Part III Discipline Engineering 100 MCQs; 85% weightage), followed by Personal Interview at Bengaluru (1:5 ratio shortlisting; 15% weightage), and Pre-Employment Medical Examination.

### F. Strategic Defence Electronics
6. **Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL — `bel-probationary-engineer`)**:
   - Navratna CPSE under the Ministry of Defence manufacturing military radar, sonar, electronic warfare, and secure communication systems.
   - Official Portal: `https://bel-india.in`.
   - Cadre: Probationary Engineer (PE) in Electronics, Mechanical, Computer Science, and Electrical disciplines.
   - Pay Scale: Grade E-II (`₹40,000 – ₹1,40,000`), Entry Basic Pay ₹40,000 during 1-yr probation. Annual CTC: approx ₹12.5 - 15 Lakhs. Canonical 58% DA constant applied.
   - Primary Documents Verified: Detailed Advertisement (`Detailed-Advertisement-1.pdf`, 13 pages) and Official PE Examination Scheme.
   - Selection Scheme: Computer Based Test (125 MCQs, 120 minutes, -0.25 negative marking; Part I Core Engineering 100 MCQs, Part II General Aptitude 25 MCQs; 85% weightage), followed by Personal Interview (1:5 ratio; 15% weightage), Document Verification, and Medical Examination.

### G. Hydrocarbon & Energy Conglomerates
7. **Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited (BPCL — `bpcl-management-trainee`)**:
   - Maharatna CPSE under the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas operating Mumbai and Kochi refineries and extensive retail fuel networks.
   - Official Portal: `https://www.bharatpetroleum.in`.
   - Cadre: Management Trainee (MT / Technical & Non-Technical).
   - Pay Scale: Grade A scale `₹50,000 – ₹1,60,000`, Entry Basic Pay ₹50,000. Annual CTC: approx ₹18 - 21 Lakhs. Canonical 58% DA constant applied.
   - Primary Sources: BPCL Official Recruitment Framework, DPE 3rd PRC IDA pay scales, and Management Trainee selection guidelines.
   - Selection Scheme: Computer Based Test (150 MCQs, 120 minutes, -0.25 negative marking; Part I General Aptitude 50 MCQs, Part II Technical/Professional Knowledge 100 MCQs), followed by Group Discussion (GD), Personal Interview (PI), and Pre-Employment Medical Examination.

8. **Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (HPCL — `hpcl-officer-engineer`)**:
   - Maharatna CPSE under the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas operating Mumbai and Visakhapatnam refineries and nation-wide pipeline networks.
   - Official Portal: `https://www.hindustanpetroleum.com`.
   - Cadre: Officer / Engineer in Mechanical, Electrical, Civil, Chemical, and Instrumentation.
   - Pay Scale: Salary Scale E2 (`₹50,000 – ₹1,60,000`), Entry Basic Pay ₹50,000. Annual CTC stated in official notification: Approx. ₹17.33 Lakhs. Canonical 58% DA constant applied.
   - Primary Document Verified: Recruitment of Officers 2024-25 Notification (`Recruitment_of_Officers_2024-25_English_05062024.pdf`, 32 pages opened and read with `pdftotext`).
   - Selection Scheme: Computer Based Test (170 MCQs, 170 marks, 150 minutes, no negative marking; Part I General Aptitude 85 MCQs, Part II Technical/Professional Knowledge 85 MCQs; 85% weightage), followed by Group Task (5% weightage), Personal Interview (10% weightage), and Pre-Employment Medical Examination.
