  - **Dearness Allowance (58%)**: ₹14,790 per month.
  - **House Rent Allowance (HRA)**: Central classification applies based on enclave posting. Puducherry urban agglomeration qualifies for Class Y rates (18%–20%, ₹4,590 to ₹5,100), while outlying enclaves (Karaikal, Mahe, Yanam) qualify for Class Z rates (9%–10%, ₹2,295 to ₹2,550).

  - Syllabus Domains: General Mathematics, General Science, General English, Indian History & Geography, Indian Economics, Constitution of India, General Knowledge & Current Affairs.
