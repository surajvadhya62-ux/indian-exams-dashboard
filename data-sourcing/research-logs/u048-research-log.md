- **Administrative & Cadre Background**:
  - Andaman & Nicobar Islands is a Union Territory directly administered by the Central Government through the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), Government of India.
  - Subordinate civil posts under the Andaman & Nicobar Administration Secretariat, Directorates, and District Establishments are Central Civil Services posts governed under Central Government rules and the 7th Central Pay Commission (7th CPC) Pay Matrix.
  - Direct recruitments for Common Higher Secondary Level (CHSLRE) and Common Graduate Level (CGL) posts are organized by the Recruitment & Examination (R&E) Cell, Secretariat, Andaman & Nicobar Administration, Sri Vijayapuram (Port Blair), frequently with technical and examination assistance from the Staff Selection Commission (SSC), New Delhi.
- **Pay Scale & Compensation Package**:
  - Foundational ministerial entry post: **Lower Grade Clerk (LGC) / Lower Division Clerk (LDC)** is positioned at **Pay Level 2** (वेतन मैट्रिक्स लेवल 02) under the Central 7th CPC Pay Matrix.
  - Pay Scale: **₹19,900 – ₹63,200** with an entry basic pay of **₹19,900** (Advt. No. 02/HSL/2026, Post Code 227/26).
  - Patwari / Village Level Worker posts: positioned at **Pay Level 3** (Entry basic pay: **₹21,700**; scale ₹21,700 – ₹69,100; Post Code 202/26).
  - Stenographer (Ordinary Grade) / Upper Division Clerk (UDC) posts: positioned at **Pay Level 4** (Entry basic pay: **₹25,500**; scale ₹25,500 – ₹81,100; Post Code 228/26).
  - Project DA Constant: Fixed strictly at **58%** as of `2025-07-01` (`da_percent_as_of_review: 58`, `da_as_of: "2025-07-01"`).
  - Island Allowances: Personnel posted in the Andaman & Nicobar Islands are entitled to the **Island Special Duty Allowance (ISDA)** under Ministry of Finance orders, ranging from 10% of Basic Pay (Port Blair / South Andaman) up to 20% of Basic Pay (difficult / remote Nicobar group of islands).
  - Other Official Perks: Government departmental quarters or House Rent Allowance (HRA), Transport Allowance (TPTA) with DA, Leave Travel Concession (LTC) including sea/air passage between mainland India (Kolkata/Chennai) and Port Blair per Central Government Island LTC rules, CGEGIS, CS(MA) medical reimbursement, and National Pension System (NPS) with 14% Central Government employer contribution.
- **Statutory Career Progression**:
  - Clerical Stream: Lower Grade Clerk (Level 2, entry basic ₹19,900) → Higher Grade Clerk / UDC (Level 4, entry basic ₹25,500; promotion after 5-8 years service or LDCE) → Assistant Section Officer / Head Clerk (Level 6, entry basic ₹35,400; ~12-16 years) → Section Officer / Office Superintendent (Level 7, entry basic ₹44,900; ~18-23 years) → Assistant Secretary / Under Secretary (Level 10, entry basic ₹56,100; ~25-30 years).
  - Revenue Stream: Patwari (Level 3, entry basic ₹21,700) → Revenue Inspector / Kanungo (Level 5) → Tehsildar / Naib Tehsildar (Level 6/7).
- **Exam Scheme & Standards**: Verified from primary recruitment notification Advt. No. 02/HSL/2026 (File No. 4-12/2026-R&E Cell/214):
  - **Stage 1: Online Tablet Based Test (TBT) / Computer Based Test (CBT)**:
    - 200 multiple-choice questions for 200 marks, duration 120 minutes (2 hours).
    - Four sections of 50 questions / 50 marks each:
      1. English Language (General English) — 50 marks
      2. General Intelligence & Reasoning — 50 marks
      3. Quantitative Aptitude — 50 marks
      4. General Awareness — 50 marks
    - Negative Marking: **0.25 marks** deducted for each wrong answer.
    - Medium: Bilingual (English and Hindi) except General English.
  - **Stage 2: Trade Test / Skill Test / Physical Endurance Test** (Qualifying in nature):
    - Computer Typing Test for Lower Grade Clerk (LDC): Qualifying speed of 35 w.p.m. in English or 30 w.p.m. in Hindi on Computer (10 minutes duration; 10,500 KDPH / 9,000 KDPH).
    - Shorthand & Typewriting Test for Stenographers: 80 w.p.m. in Shorthand and 40 w.p.m. on typewriter or 45 w.p.m. on computer.
    - Physical Endurance Test for Foresters and field cadres.
  - Stage 3: Document Verification on original testimonials.

  - **2022 Cycle (F.No. 1-3/2022-R&E Cell dated 26/08/2022)**: **565 vacancies** notified in revised vacancy position (UR: 274, ST: 54, OBC: 186, EWS: 53, PwD: 12, MSP: 27, ESM: 09; including 453 LGCs in Secretariat, 37 Stenographers, 8 Patwaris, 19 Tally Clerks, and 18 Village Level Workers).
