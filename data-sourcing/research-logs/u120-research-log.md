## 1. Administrative Framework & Statutory Authority

The **Goa Staff Selection Commission (GSSC)** is the statutory recruiting body constituted by the Government of Goa to conduct examinations and select candidates for appointment to all subordinate services and posts (Group 'C' posts) across departments of the Government of Goa.

- **Headquarters:** 6th Floor, 3rd Lift, SPACES Building, Patto Plaza, Panaji, Goa – 403 001
- **Official Domain:** `https://gssc.goa.gov.in`
- **Online Application / Examination Portal:** `https://gssc.goa.gov.in`
- **Advertisements Hub:** `https://gssc.goa.gov.in/?page_id=1079`
- **Syllabus & Scheme Hub:** `https://gssc.goa.gov.in/?page_id=1096`
- **Sample Question Papers Hub:** `https://gssc.goa.gov.in/?page_id=1383`
- **Results & Selection Lists Hub:** `https://gssc.goa.gov.in/?page_id=1230`

### Statutory Instruments Governing Recruitment:
1. **The Goa Staff Selection Commission Act, 2019 (Goa Act 11 of 2019):** Passed by the Goa Legislative Assembly to mandate centralized recruitment for all Group C posts in the state government.
2. **The Goa Staff Selection Commission (Amendment) Acts:** Goa Act 17 of 2024 and earlier 2022 amendments operationalizing the commission's testing infrastructure.
3. **GSSC Examination & Procedure Regulations:** The Goa Staff Selection Commission (Examinations, Selection of candidates to the sub-ordinate services posts and Procedure for conduct of the business of the Commission) Regulations, 2023, as amended by the Third Amendment Regulations, 2026.
4. **Domicile & Language Prerequisite:** 15 years continuous residence in the State of Goa (Residence Certificate issued by Mamlatdar) and essential knowledge of Konkani language (with Marathi desirable).
5. **State Pay Revision Framework (§EXECUTION-PLAN 5.3):** The Government of Goa adopts the Central Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 2016 for state employees vide Government of Goa, Finance (Rev. & Cont.) Department Order No. 8/1/2016-Fin(R&C). Central 7th CPC matrix Level 2 (entry basic ₹19,900) applies to LDC / Junior Assistant / Recovery Clerk, while Level 4 (entry basic ₹25,500) applies to Accounts Clerk.

### Selection Architecture (Two-Tier CBT + Skill Test):
Verified directly from GSSC's *Syllabus and Scheme of Examination — Advertisement No. 3 of Year 2026* and *Advertisement No. 3 of Year 2025*:
- **Stage 1: CBT-I (Computer Based Screening Examination)**
  - 60 Objective MCQs, 60 marks, 75 minutes composite duration.
  - Sections:
    1. Test of English & Comprehension (20 marks)
    2. Test of Reasoning (20 marks)
    3. Test of Numerical Ability (10 marks)
    4. Test of Konkani Language (10 marks)
  - **Crucial Statutory Threshold:** Candidates must score at least 4 out of 10 marks in the Konkani Language section (questions 1 to 10). Only candidates scoring ≥ 4 marks in Konkani language have the remainder of their paper evaluated!
  - Minimum overall passing in CBT-I: 45% for Unreserved (27/60), 40% for OBC (24/60), and 35% for SC/ST/PwD (21/60).
  - Shortlisting ratio: Top 5 times the vacancies in each category are shortlisted for CBT-II.
  - CBT-I is strictly screening/qualifying in nature; marks are not counted towards final selection merit.
  - Negative marking: None.
- **Stage 2: CBT-II (Computer Based Merit Examination)**
  - 80 Objective MCQs, 80 marks, 100 minutes duration.
  - Sections:
    1. Test of English & Comprehension (30 marks)
    2. Test of Reasoning (20 marks)
    3. Test of Basic Knowledge of Computer Proficiency (20 marks)
    4. Test of Numerical Ability (10 marks)
  - Minimum qualifying marks: 45% for UR (36/80), 40% for OBC (32/80), 35% for SC/ST/PwD (28/80).
  - **100% Final Merit:** Selection is determined strictly by aggregate marks scored in CBT-II.
  - Negative marking: None.
- **Stage 3: Skill / Typing Test**
  - Mandatory qualifying English typing speed test of 30 words per minute (wpm) on computer for LDC, Recovery Clerk, Junior Assistant, and Data Entry Operator cadres.
  - Shortlisting: Candidates who pass CBT-II or 2 times the vacancy count in each category.
  - Strictly qualifying in nature; does not alter CBT-II merit ranking.

### Financial Package (State Rule §5.3):
- **Pay Scale:** Appointed under CCS (Revised Pay) Rules 2016 as adopted by Government of Goa at **Pay Matrix Level 2 (₹19,900 – ₹63,200)** for LDC/Clerk/Junior Assistant, and **Level 4 (₹25,500 – ₹81,100)** for Accounts Clerk.
- **Entry Basic Pay:** **₹19,900**.
- **Project Canonical Constant DA:** 58% as of 2025-07-01 (₹11,542).
- **House Rent Allowance:** 10% to 20% (₹1,990 to ₹3,980) based on location classification across North Goa / South Goa municipal areas and rural talukas.
- **Transport Allowance:** ₹1,350 + 58% DA = ₹2,133.
- **Estimated Gross Range:** ₹35,000 – ₹39,000 per month.
- **Estimated Net In-Hand Range:** ₹31,000 – ₹35,000 per month (after statutory deductions including 10% NPS basic+DA = ₹3,144, Goa Professional Tax ₹200/month, and GIS).

### Promotional Hierarchy (Career Ladder):
- **Entry:** Lower Division Clerk / Junior Assistant / Recovery Clerk (Pay Level 2, ₹19,900)
- **Rung 2:** Upper Division Clerk (UDC) (Pay Level 4, ₹25,500; approx. 8 years service in Level 2 or departmental exam)
- **Rung 3:** Head Clerk / Assistant / Accountant (Pay Level 6, ₹35,400; approx. 5–7 years in Level 4)
- **Rung 4:** Superintendent / Section Officer (Group B Gazetted) (Pay Level 7, ₹44,900; approx. 3–5 years in Level 6)
- **Apex:** Under Secretary / Junior Scale Officer (Goa Civil Service) (Pay Level 10, ₹56,100; promotion quota induction into state civil service)
