### Constitutional & Administrative Framework:
- **Territorial Administration & Police Authority**:
  - The Union Territory of Puducherry is governed under **Article 239A of the Constitution of India** and the **Government of Union Territories Act, 1963**, administered under the supervisory oversight of the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), Government of India.
  - The Puducherry Police Department is headed by the Director General of Police (DGP), Puducherry, operating police stations and specialized wings (Law & Order, Traffic, Coastal Security Police, Armed Reserve, and Crime Branch) across Puducherry enclave, Karaikal (enclave within Tamil Nadu), Mahe (enclave on the Malabar Coast of Kerala), and Yanam (enclave on the Godavari delta of Andhra Pradesh).
  - Recruitment is conducted centrally through the **Puducherry Examining Authority (PEA)** / DP&AR and the Police Department Recruitment Board.
- **Domicile & Nativity Prerequisite**:
  - Candidates must be Indian citizens and residents of the Union Territory of Puducherry who have resided in the UT continuously for not less than **five years** immediately preceding the cut-off date (attested by a continuous residence certificate from a Competent Authority/Tahsildar).
- **Linguistic Inclusivity**:
  - In recognition of Puducherry's four linguistically distinct territories, the competitive written examination is provided bilingually in English alongside the candidate's chosen regional language: **Tamil** (for Puducherry and Karaikal), **Malayalam** (for Mahe), or **Telugu** (for Yanam).

### Examination Scheme & Multi-Stage Selection Architecture:
The selection process is governed by the Puducherry Police Subordinate Services Recruitment Rules and conducted in three sequential stages:
1. **Stage 1: Physical Standard Test (PST) & Physical Efficiency Test (PET)** (Mandatory & Qualifying):
   - **PST Standards**:
     - *Male*: Minimum Height 165 cm; Chest 81 cm (unexpanded) with minimum 5 cm expansion (86 cm); Body Mass Index (BMI) 18.50 to 29.99 kg/m².
     - *Female*: Minimum Height 154 cm; Body Weight minimum 45 kg; Body Mass Index (BMI) 18.50 to 29.99 kg/m².
   - **PET Events** (All events compulsory and qualifying):
     - *Male*: (i) 800 Metres Run in 2 minutes 50 seconds; (ii) Long Jump 3.80 metres (3 attempts); (iii) High Jump 1.20 metres (3 attempts); (iv) 100 Metres Run in 15.00 seconds.
     - *Female*: (i) 200 Metres Run in 45.00 seconds; (ii) Long Jump 2.75 metres (3 attempts); (iii) High Jump 0.90 metres (3 attempts).
2. **Stage 2: Written Competitive Examination** (200 Marks Total, Merit Determining):
   - Standard: Higher Secondary (10+2) Standard.
   - Format: Two Objective OMR Papers of 100 questions / 100 marks each (120 minutes each).
   - Negative Marking: 0.25 marks deducted per wrong answer.
   - Languages: Bilingual (English + Tamil / Telugu / Malayalam).
   - **Paper-I** (100 Marks, 120 Mins): General Intelligence & Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, General Awareness & Current Affairs, English Language, Basic Computer Operations.
   - **Paper-II** (100 Marks, 120 Mins): Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics) and Social Sciences (History of India & World, Geography, Indian Polity & Constitution, Economics & Indian Economy).
   - Minimum Qualifying Cut-off Thresholds: Must appear and qualify in both papers:
     - Unreserved (UR): 30%
     - Most Backward Classes (MBC) / OBC / EWS / EBC / BCM / BT: 25%
     - Scheduled Castes (SC) / Scheduled Tribes (ST) / Ex-Servicemen (XSM): 20%
3. **Stage 3: Certificate Verification & Medical Examination** (Qualifying):
   - Scrutiny of 10+2 educational qualifications, continuous 5-year Puducherry residence/nativity certificate, community/caste certificates, and horizontal quota entitlements (Women 33%, XSM 10%, Meritorious Sports Persons / MSP 5%).
   - Medical Fitness Examination conducted by the Government Medical Board at General Hospital, Puducherry (Vision 6/6 right eye, 6/9 left eye without glasses; normal color perception; free from knock-knees, flat foot, and physical infirmities).
   - Selected candidates undergo 2 years of probation, including 1 year of compulsory basic training at the Police Training School, Puducherry.
