## 3. Detailed Examination Research Profiles

### 3.1. Nagaland Staff Selection Board CSEE / CSRE (`nssb-csee`)
- **Statutory Authority**: Nagaland Staff Selection Board (NSSB) constituted under the *Nagaland Staff Selection Board Regulations, 2020* (notified vide No. AR-3/Gen-374/2020 dated 20th July 2020).
- **Official Portal**: `https://nssb.nagaland.gov.in` (HTTP 200).
- **Scope & Posts**: Centralized recruitment for all Group-C direct recruitment vacancies across Nagaland state directorates, including Junior Divisional Accountant, LDA cum Computer Assistant, Storekeeper, Assistant Jailor, and Sub-Inspector of Excise.
- **Pay Scale**: Nagaland Services (Revision of Pay) Rules 2017 (ROP 2017) Level 5 (₹25,500 – ₹81,100). Entry basic pay: ₹25,500.
- **Financial Architecture**:
  - DA @ 58%: ₹14,790.
  - HRA (Kohima/Dimapur 8%): ₹2,040.
  - Special Duty Allowance (SDA 10%): ₹2,550.
  - Medical Allowance: ₹1,000.
  - Estimated Gross Monthly: ₹43,000 – ₹48,000; In-hand: ₹39,000 – ₹44,000.
- **Career Ladder**: Junior Divisional Accountant / LDA cum Computer Assistant (Level 5) -> Senior Divisional Accountant (Level 7) -> Divisional Accounts Officer / Superintendent (Level 10) -> Senior Accounts Officer / Assistant Director (Level 12) -> Joint Director (Level 13).
- **Exam Pattern**:
  - General Paper Written Examination (200 marks, 200 MCQs, 3 hours):
    - General English (50 marks)
    - General Knowledge (50 marks)
    - Elementary Mathematics & Reasoning (50 marks)
    - Nagaland State Specific GK & Customary Heritage (50 marks)
    - Negative marking: 1/3rd (0.33 mark per wrong answer).
  - Skill Test: Computer Practical & Typing Speed Test (30 wpm, qualifying).
  - Viva-Voce: 25 marks (where prescribed by service rules, up to 12.5% of written marks).
- **Primary Source Citations**:
  - `https://nssb.nagaland.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/NSSB-General-Paper-Syllabus.pdf` (General Paper Common Syllabus)
  - `https://nssb.nagaland.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/NSSB-Regulations-2020.pdf` (NSSB Regulations 2020)

### 3.2. Mizoram Subordinate Services Selection Board Combined Group 'C' (`msssb-group-c`)
- **Statutory Authority**: Mizoram Subordinate Services Selection Board (MSSSB) established under DP&AR Notification `No. A.12018/1/2019-P&AR(GSW)` dated 10th June 2019 and *The Mizoram Subordinate Services Selection Board Regulations, 2019*.
- **Official Portal**: `https://dpar.mizoram.gov.in` and `https://dipr.mizoram.gov.in` (HTTP 200).
- **Scope & Posts**: Direct recruitment for Lower Division Clerk (LDC), Subordinate Assistant, and Group-C ministerial cadres across Mizoram Civil Secretariat and state directorates.
- **Pay Scale**: Mizoram Services (Revision of Pay) Rules 2018 (ROP 2018) Level 4 (₹25,500 – ₹56,800). Entry basic pay: ₹25,500.
- **Financial Architecture**:
  - DA @ 58%: ₹14,790.
  - HRA (8% Aizawl/districts): ₹2,040.
  - Hill / Remote Compensatory Allowance: ₹1,500.
  - Medical Allowance: ₹1,000.
  - Estimated Gross Monthly: ₹42,000 – ₹47,000; In-hand: ₹38,000 – ₹43,000.
- **Career Ladder**: Lower Division Clerk (Level 4) -> Upper Division Clerk (Level 6) -> Assistant / Head Assistant (Level 7) -> Superintendent (Level 8) -> Under Secretary (Level 10).
- **Exam Pattern**:
  - Written Competitive Examination (200 marks, 2 papers of 2 hours each):
    - Paper I: General English (100 marks, Essay, Précis, Grammar, Comprehension)
    - Paper II: General Studies, Arithmetic & Basic Computer Knowledge (100 marks)
  - Skill Test: Typing Speed Test (30 wpm in English on Computer) and NIELIT Course on Computer Concepts (CCC) certificate scrutiny.
  - Document Verification & Merit List.
- **Primary Source Citations**:
  - `https://dpar.mizoram.gov.in` (DPAR Recruitment Rules & Schemes)
  - `https://dipr.mizoram.gov.in` (Mizoram DIPR Recruitment Bulletins & Shortlists)

### 3.3. Manipur Police Sub-Inspector & Jamadar (`manipur-police-si`)
- **Statutory Authority**: Police Department, Government of Manipur / State Police Recruitment Board under the *Manipur Police Act* and executive cadre recruitment rules.
- **Official Portal**: `https://manipurpolice.gov.in` (HTTP 200).
- **Scope & Posts**: Sub-Inspector of Police (Executive / Civil Police) and Jamadar (Manipur Rifles / India Reserve Battalions).
- **Pay Scale**: Manipur Services (Revised Pay) Rules 2019 (MS ROP 2019) Level 6 (₹35,400 – ₹1,12,400). Entry basic pay: ₹35,400.
- **Financial Architecture**:
  - DA @ 58%: ₹20,532.
  - HRA (8% Imphal/districts): ₹2,832.
  - Police Special Duty & Risk Allowance: ₹3,000.
  - Subsidized Ration & Kit Maintenance: ₹2,500.
  - Medical Allowance: ₹1,000.
  - Estimated Gross Monthly: ₹61,000 – ₹67,000; In-hand: ₹54,000 – ₹60,000.
- **Career Ladder**: Sub-Inspector / Jamadar (Level 6) -> Inspector / Subedar (Level 7) -> Deputy Superintendent of Police (DSP / Assistant Commandant, Level 10, MPS Cadre) -> Addl. SP (Level 11) -> Superintendent of Police (Level 12 / 13) with IPS induction opportunity.
- **Exam Pattern**:
  - Stage 1: PST & PET (1600m run in 6m30s, 100m sprint in 15s, High Jump 1.20m, Long Jump 4.25m, Shot Put; qualifying).
  - Stage 2: Written Examination (100 marks, 3 hours):
    - General Knowledge & Current Affairs (40 marks)
    - General English (30 marks)
    - Elementary Mathematics & Logical Reasoning (30 marks)
  - Stage 3: Viva-Voce / Personality Test (15 marks).
  - Stage 4: Medical Examination & Police Antecedents Verification.
- **Primary Source Citations**:
  - `https://manipurpolice.gov.in` (Manipur Police Official Portal & Recruitment Schedules)

### 3.4. Meghalaya Police Central Recruitment Board MLPRB (`meghalaya-police-mlp`)
- **Statutory Authority**: Central Recruitment Board (CRB), Meghalaya Police Department, Government of Meghalaya.
- **Official Portal**: `https://megpolice.gov.in` (HTTP 200).
- **Scope & Posts**: Unarmed Branch Constable (UBC), Armed Branch Constable (ABC), Fireman, MPRO Operator, and Unarmed Branch Sub-Inspector (UB SI).
- **Pay Scale**: Meghalaya Services (Revision of Pay) Rules 2018:
  - Constable / Fireman / MPRO: Level 3 (₹20,600 – ₹47,000), entry basic ₹20,600.
  - Sub-Inspector (UB SI / AB SI): Level 11 (₹37,800 – ₹86,400), entry basic ₹37,800.
- **Financial Architecture** (Constable Level 3 benchmark):
  - DA @ 58%: ₹11,948.
  - HRA (8% Shillong/districts): ₹1,648.
  - Hill Compensatory & Winter Allowance: ₹1,200.
  - Ration & Kit Maintenance Allowance: ₹2,000.
  - Estimated Gross Monthly: ₹35,000 – ₹40,000; In-hand: ₹31,000 – ₹36,000.
- **Career Ladder**: Constable (Level 3) -> Head Constable (Level 5) -> Assistant Sub-Inspector (Level 8) -> Sub-Inspector (Level 11) -> Inspector (Level 15) -> DSP (Level 16, MPS Cadre).
- **Exam Pattern**:
  - Stage 1: Physical Efficiency Test (PET) with RFID chip timing (5 km in 24 mins for male, 3 km in 19 mins for female; up to 100 marks for UBC / 250 marks for ABC).
  - Stage 2: Written Examination (300 marks UBC / 150 marks ABC / 300 marks SI):
    - General English (100 marks)
    - Elementary Mathematics & Basic Science (100 marks)
    - General Knowledge & Reasoning (100 marks)
  - Stage 3: Viva-Voce / Personal Interview (50 marks).
  - Stage 4: Medical Examination & Character Scrutiny.
- **Primary Source Citations**:
  - `https://megpolice.gov.in/sites/default/files/7.un-armed-branch-constable-dtd-25.02.2026.pdf` (UBC Merit Notice & Pay Schedule)
  - `https://megpolice.gov.in/sites/default/files/selectlistubsidt.08.12.2025.pdf` (UB SI Selection Notice)
  - `https://megpolice.gov.in` (Meghalaya Police CRB Portal)

### 3.5. Tripura Police Constable (`tripura-police-constable`)
- **Statutory Authority**: Tripura Police Department, Office of the Director General of Police, Government of Tripura, Agartala under the *Tripura Police Act, 2007*.
- **Official Portal**: `https://police.tripura.gov.in` (Verified HTTP 200; note domain distinction from `tripurapolice.gov.in`).
- **Scope & Posts**: Police Constable (Men & Women, Executive Branch) and Rifleman in the Tripura State Rifles (TSR).
- **Pay Scale**: Tripura State Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules 2018 Pay Matrix Level 6 (₹20,900 – ₹66,400). Entry basic pay: ₹20,900.
- **Financial Architecture**:
  - DA @ 58%: ₹12,122.
  - HRA (8% Agartala/districts): ₹1,672.
  - Police Ration Allowance: ₹2,000.
  - Uniform & Police Duty Allowance: ₹1,500.
  - Medical Allowance: ₹1,000.
  - Estimated Gross Monthly: ₹36,000 – ₹41,000; In-hand: ₹32,000 – ₹37,000.
- **Career Ladder**: Police Constable / TSR Rifleman (Level 6) -> Head Constable (Level 7) -> Assistant Sub-Inspector (Level 8) -> Sub-Inspector of Police (Level 10) -> Inspector of Police (Level 12) -> Deputy Superintendent of Police (Level 14, TPS Cadre).
- **Exam Pattern**:
  - Stage 1: PST & PET (Endurance Run: 4 km for male candidates, 1.6 km for female candidates; qualifying).
  - Stage 2: Written Competitive Examination (85 marks, 85 MCQs, 90 minutes):
    - General Intelligence & Reasoning (25 marks)
    - General Knowledge & Current Affairs including Tripura (25 marks)
    - Elementary Mathematics (20 marks)
    - Language Comprehension (Bengali / Kokborok / English) (15 marks)
    - Negative marking: 0.25 mark per incorrect answer.
  - Stage 3: Viva-Voce / Interview (15 marks).
  - Stage 4: Medical Examination & Police Antecedents Verification.
- **Primary Source Citations**:
  - `https://police.tripura.gov.in` (Tripura Police Official Portal)
  - Notifications and Physical Standards issued by Police Headquarters, Agartala.
