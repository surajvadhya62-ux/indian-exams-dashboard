## 1. Administrative Framework & Statutory Authority

Recruitment to subordinate Group 'C' executive, administrative, developmental, and ministerial cadres across the State of Uttarakhand is conducted through:

### A. Uttarakhand Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UKSSSC)
- **Headquarters**: Near Maharana Pratap Sports College, Thano Road, Raipur, Dehradun, Uttarakhand – 248 008
- **Official Domain**: `https://sssc.uk.gov.in`
- **Statutory Authority**:
  1. **Uttarakhand Subordinate Service Selection Commission Act, 2014 (Uttarakhand Act No. 20 of 2014)**: Enacted by the Uttarakhand State Legislature to constitute a selection commission for direct recruitment to Group 'C' posts across state departments and public undertakings.
  2. **The Uttarakhand Procedure for Direct Recruitment to Group 'C' Posts (Outside the Purview of the Public Service Commission) Rules, 2008** and Government Order No. 220/XXX(4)/2017 dated 19 July 2017.
  3. **Government of Uttarakhand Personnel and Vigilance Department (कार्मिक एवं सतर्कता अनुभाग-4)**:
     - Order No. `426609/XXX(4)/2026-03(03)/26-E-94441` dated 25.08.2026: Approved revised examination scheme and syllabus for Uttarakhand Subordinate Office Graduate Level (Group 'C') Service Examination.
     - Order No. `426610/XXX(4)/2026-03(03)/26-E-94441` dated 25.08.2026: Approved revised examination scheme and syllabus for Uttarakhand Subordinate Office Clerical Cadre (Junior Assistant / Other Intermediate Level) Service Examination.

### B. State Pay Structure & Policy Framework (§EXECUTION-PLAN 5.3)
- **State Pay Revision Framework**: Employees of the Government of Uttarakhand are governed by the **Uttarakhand Revised Pay Structure Rules, 2016** (Finance Department GO No. 138/XXVII(7)40(2016)/2016 dated 23 December 2016 adapting the 7th Central Pay Commission matrix for Uttarakhand State Services).
- **Pay Level Allocation**:
  - Village Development Officer (VDO) / Village Panchayat Development Officer (VPDO): Pay Level 4 (Entry Basic ₹25,500; Scale ₹25,500 – ₹81,100).
  - Assistant Social Welfare Officer / Senior VDO: Pay Level 5 (Entry Basic ₹29,200; Scale ₹29,200 – ₹92,300).
  - Junior Assistant (कनिष्ठ सहायक) / Data Entry Operator (DEO) / Tax Collector (कर संग्रहकर्ता): Pay Level 3 (Entry Basic ₹21,700; Scale ₹21,700 – ₹69,100).
- **Allowances in Uttarakhand**:
  - Dearness Allowance (DA): Canonical project constant of **58%** as of `2025-07-01`.
  - House Rent Allowance (HRA): 9% in rural/remote mountain blocks, up to 18% in urban municipal areas (such as Dehradun).
  - Hill Compensatory Allowance (HCA): Admissible for notified high-altitude mountain blocks and border district postings.
  - Fixed Medical Allowance (FMA): ₹1,000/month or comprehensive coverage under Uttarakhand State Health Schemes (USHS).
  - Deductions: National Pension System (NPS) statutory 10% on Basic + DA, plus Uttarakhand State Group Insurance (GIS ~₹200).
