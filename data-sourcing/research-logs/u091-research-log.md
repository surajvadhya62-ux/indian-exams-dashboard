## 3. Detailed Research Logs

### 3.1 Gujarat Police Sub-Inspector (`gujarat-police-psi`)
- **Statutory Framework**: Governed by the Gujarat Police Act, 1951 and the Gujarat Police Sub-Inspector (Recruitment) Rules.
- **Selection Architecture**:
  - Stage 1: PET/PST — 5,000m run in 25 mins (Male), 1,600m in 9m 30s (Female); qualifying height & chest.
  - Stage 2: Prelims MCQ — 100 marks, 120 mins, 0.25 negative marking.
  - Stage 3: Mains Written Exam — 4 papers (400 marks total): Paper 1 Gujarati (100m), Paper 2 English (100m), Paper 3 General Studies (100m), Paper 4 Legal Aspects & Criminal Law (IPC/BNS, CrPC/BNSS, BSA, Gujarat Police Act, Prohibition Act, Atrocities Act) (100m).
  - Stage 4: Document Verification and Medical Board.
- **Compensation & Fix Pay Note**: In Gujarat, Class-3 recruits initially draw fixed pay for a 5-year probation (enhanced to ₹38,090/month). Upon regularization, incumbents transition to 7th CPC Level 7 (Basic ₹39,900). At 58% DA, gross pay reaches ₹72,000–₹78,500.

### 3.2 GSSSB Combined Competitive Examination (`gsssb-cce-clerk`)
- **Statutory Framework**: Gujarat Subordinate Services Class-III (Combined Competitive Examination) Rules, 2023 (Notification No. GS/2023/27/CRR/102023/339/G.2).
- **Selection Architecture**:
  - Stage 1: Preliminary CBRT (100 MCQs, 60 mins, 0.25 negative marking) covering Reasoning (40m), Quantitative Aptitude (30m), Gujarati Comprehension (15m), and English Comprehension (15m). Minimum qualifying: 40%.
  - Stage 2: Group A Mains (Descriptive, 350 marks: Gujarati 100m, English 100m, GS 150m); Group B Mains (Objective CBRT, 200 marks: 120 mins, 7 subjects).
  - Stage 3: Document Verification.
- **Compensation**: Junior Clerk entry basic ₹19,950 (Level 2) with fixed pay of ₹26,000/month during 5-year probation. Senior Clerk entry basic ₹25,500 (Level 4).

### 3.3 GPSSB/GSSSB Talati cum Mantri (`gsssb-talati-mantri`)
- **Statutory Framework**: Gujarat Panchayat Service (Selection) Rules under Gujarat Panchayats Act, 1993.
- **Selection Architecture**:
  - Single-stage OMR Written Examination (100 questions, 100 marks, 60 minutes).
  - Syllabus: General Awareness & GK (50m), Gujarati Language & Grammar (20m), English Language & Grammar (20m), General Mathematics (10m). Negative marking: 0.33 marks (Option E avoids deduction).
  - Stage 2: Document Verification based on statewide merit.
- **Competition Benchmark**: In 2023 (Advt 10/2021-22), 17,10,000 registered applicants, 8,65,000 appeared on a single day for 3,437 vacancies.

### 3.4 JKSSB Panchayat Secretary (`jkssb-panchayat-secretary`)
- **Statutory Framework**: Department of Rural Development & Panchayati Raj, J&K under J&K Panchayati Raj Act, 1989 and Notification No. 03 of 2022.
- **Selection Architecture**:
  - Single-stage OMR Written Exam (120 marks, 120 minutes, 0.25 negative marking).
  - Six components: J&K GK (20m), English (20m), Reasoning (20m), Quantitative Aptitude (15m), Computers (25m), Basic Maths & Statistics (20m).
  - Stage 2: Document Verification for district-cadre allocation.
- **Compensation**: Level 2 (₹19,900–₹63,200) under S.O. 192 of 2020. Full basic pay + allowances drawn from inception.

### 3.5 JKSSB Accounts Assistant (`jkssb-accounts-assistant`)
- **Statutory Framework**: Finance Department and Rural Development Department, Government of J&K under Jammu & Kashmir Subordinate Accounts Service Rules.
- **Selection Architecture**:
  - Single-stage OMR Exam (120 marks, 120 minutes, 0.25 negative marking).
  - Syllabus: J&K GK (30m), Accountancy & Book Keeping (30m), English (10m), Statistics (10m), Mathematics (10m), Economics (10m), General Science (10m), Computers (10m).
  - Stage 2: Document Verification.
- **Compensation**: Level 5 (₹29,200–₹92,300) with gross pay ₹51,500–₹56,500 at 58% DA.

### 3.6 JKSSB Junior Assistant (`jkssb-junior-assistant`)
- **Statutory Framework**: General Administration Department, Government of J&K Ministerial Staff (Subordinate) Service Rules.
- **Selection Architecture**:
  - Stage 1: Computer Based Type Test (Qualifying, minimum 35 wpm with 90% accuracy).
  - Stage 2: Written Examination (80 marks, 80 mins, 0.25 negative marking: English 20m, J&K GK 20m, Reasoning/Numerical 20m, Computers 20m).
  - Stage 3: Document Verification.
- **Compensation**: Level 4 (₹25,500–₹81,100) with gross pay ₹45,000–₹49,500.

### 3.7 Karnataka Police Constable (`ksp-police-constable`)
- **Statutory Framework**: Karnataka State Police (Cadre and Recruitment) Rules.
- **Selection Architecture**:
  - Stage 1: Written Examination (100 marks, 90 mins, bilingual Kannada & English, 0.25 negative marking). Shortlists 1:5 for physical tests.
  - Stage 2: Endurance Test & Physical Standard Test (Male: 1,600m run in 6m 30s, Long/High Jump, Shot Put; Female: 1,000m run in 6 mins, Long/High Jump, Shot Put).
  - Stage 3: Document Verification & Medical Examination.
- **Compensation**: 7th State Pay Commission scale ₹23,500–₹47,650 (Basic ₹23,500). Gross pay: ₹43,500–₹49,500.

### 3.8 Karnataka Police Sub-Inspector (`ksp-psi`)
- **Statutory Framework**: Karnataka State Police Services (Recruitment) Rules.
- **Selection Architecture**:
  - Stage 1: Endurance Test & Physical Standard Test (Qualifying; Male 1,600m in 7 mins, Female 400m in 2 mins, Long/High jump, Shot put, Height/Chest).
  - Stage 2: Written Examination (Total 200 marks): Paper 1 Descriptive (50 marks, 90 mins: Essay 20m, Translation 20m, Précis 10m); Paper 2 Objective MCQ (150 marks, 100 questions, 90 mins, 0.375 negative marking).
  - Stage 3: Document Verification & Medical Board.
- **Compensation**: State Pay Scale ₹37,900–₹70,850 (Basic ₹37,900). Gross pay: ₹68,500–₹75,500.
