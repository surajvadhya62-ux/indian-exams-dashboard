## 3. Detailed Exam Research Logs

### 3.1 KEA Village Administrative Officer (`kea-vao`)
- **Conducting Authority**: Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) for the Revenue Department, Government of Karnataka (`https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea/`).
- **Statutory Framework**: Karnataka Revenue Subordinate Service Cadre and Recruitment Rules, Karnataka Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, and KEA Direct Recruitment Regulations (Advt. No. KEA/AD/26/RECT/2026).
- **Career Trajectory**: Village Administrative Officer (VAO / Grama Prashasana Adhikari, Scale ₹21,400–₹42,000) → Revenue Inspector (RI / Hobli In-charge, Scale ₹27,650–₹52,650) → Deputy Tahsildar (DT / Nadakacheri Head, Scale ₹37,900–₹70,850) → Tahsildar (Taluk Magistrate / Group B Gazetted, Scale ₹43,100–₹83,900) → Assistant Commissioner (Sub-Divisional Magistrate / KAS Senior Time Scale, Scale ₹67,550–₹1,04,600).
- **Exam Scheme**:
  - Stage 1: Compulsory Kannada Language Examination (Kaddaya Kannada, 150 marks, 120 mins; qualifying 35% / 50 marks, SSLC standard, exempted for candidates with Kannada as 1st/2nd language).
  - Stage 2: Competitive Written Examination (Offline OMR, 200 marks): Paper 1 General Knowledge (100 marks, 120 mins, -0.25 negative marking); Paper 2 General Kannada / General English and Computer Knowledge (100 marks, 120 mins, -0.25 negative marking).
  - Stage 3: Document Verification & Merit Allotment.
- **Compensation**: Entry Basic Pay ₹21,400 (Scale ₹21,400–₹42,000, Group C Revenue Cadre). At 58% DA (₹12,412) + HRA (9% to 27%), estimated gross pay is ₹35,000–₹41,000 and in-hand pay is ₹31,000–₹36,000 (after NPS, KGID, PT deductions).
- **Competition Benchmarks**: 2026 cycle: 1,000 vacancies (Residual Parent Cadre and Kalyana Karnataka Cadre) with over 5,70,000 registered candidates across Karnataka.

### 3.2 KPTCL Junior Engineer (`kptcl-junior-engineer`)
- **Conducting Authority**: Karnataka Power Transmission Corporation Limited (KPTCL) (`https://kptcl.karnataka.gov.in`).
- **Statutory Framework**: KPTCL Cadre & Recruitment Regulations, State Power Utility Board Revised Pay Scales, and Notification No. KPTCL/B16/21723/2021-22.
- **Career Trajectory**: Junior Engineer (JE Electrical / Civil, Scale ₹26,270–₹65,020) → Assistant Engineer (AE, Scale ₹41,130–₹72,920) → Assistant Executive Engineer (AEE / Sub-Station Head, Scale ₹57,120–₹1,06,860) → Executive Engineer (EE / Division Head, Scale ₹74,400–₹1,12,800) → Superintending Engineer (SE / Circle Head, Scale ₹90,500–₹1,23,300) → Chief Engineer (CE / SLDC / Zonal Grid, Scale ₹1,04,600–₹1,41,300).
- **Exam Scheme**:
  - Stage 1: Compulsory Kannada Language Test (150 marks, 120 mins; qualifying 35%, SSLC standard).
  - Stage 2: Aptitude Test (OMR, 100 marks, 120 mins, -0.25 negative marking): 75 marks Core Technical Diploma Engineering curriculum + 25 marks General Aptitude and Awareness.
  - Stage 3: Document Verification and Medical Fitness Examination.
- **Compensation**: Entry Basic Pay ₹26,270 (Scale ₹26,270–₹65,020, Supervisory Engineering Cadre). At 58% DA (₹15,237) + HRA (9% to 27%), estimated gross pay is ₹44,000–₹52,000 and in-hand pay is ₹39,000–₹46,000.
- **Competition Benchmarks**: In the direct recruitment cycle, 599 JE posts (570 Electrical, 29 Civil) attracted 1,42,850 candidates.

### 3.3 Karnataka Forest Guard (`karnataka-forest-guard`)
- **Conducting Authority**: Karnataka Forest Department (KFD) (`https://aranya.gov.in`, `https://recruitment.aranya.gov.in`).
- **Statutory Framework**: Karnataka Forest Department Subordinate Services Cadre & Recruitment Rules and KCSR Revised Pay Scales.
- **Career Trajectory**: Forest Guard (Aranya Rakshaka / Beat Forester, Scale ₹21,400–₹42,000) → Deputy Range Forest Officer (DRFO / Section Forester, Scale ₹27,650–₹52,650) → Range Forest Officer (RFO / Territorial & Wildlife Range In-charge, Scale ₹43,100–₹83,900, Group B Gazetted) → Assistant Conservator of Forests (ACF, Scale ₹52,650–₹97,100) → Deputy Conservator of Forests (DCF / DFO, Scale ₹67,550–₹1,04,600).
- **Exam Scheme**:
  - Stage 1: Physical Standard Test (PST) & Physical Endurance Test (PET) (Qualifying events: 1600m/1000m run, sprint, jumps, shot put) + Endurance Walking Test (25 km in 4 hours for men; 14 km in 4 hours for women).
  - Stage 2: Written Aptitude Examination (Offline OMR, 100 marks, 120 mins, -0.25 negative marking): Mathematics (SSLC standard, 40 marks) and General Knowledge, Forestry, Wildlife & Environment (60 marks).
  - Stage 3: Medical Board Fitness & Document Scrutiny.
- **Compensation**: Entry Basic Pay ₹21,400 (Scale ₹21,400–₹42,000). At 58% DA (₹12,412) + HRA + forest beat/uniform allowances, estimated gross pay is ₹35,000–₹41,000 and in-hand pay is ₹31,000–₹36,000.
- **Competition Benchmarks**: 540 vacancies across 13 territorial circles attracted 1,85,000 applicants with 1:20 shortlisted for physical endurance tests.

### 3.4 Karnataka Judicial Service (`karnataka-judicial-service`)
- **Conducting Authority**: High Court of Karnataka (`https://karnatakajudiciary.kar.nic.in`).
- **Statutory Framework**: Karnataka Judicial Service (Recruitment) Rules, 2004 under High Court of Karnataka superintendence, with pay scales governed by the Second National Judicial Pay Commission (SNJPC).
- **Career Trajectory**: Civil Judge (Junior Division) / JMFC (SNJPC J-1 ₹77,840–₹1,36,520) → Civil Judge (Senior Stage / ACP 1, J-2 ₹92,960–₹1,36,520) → Senior Civil Judge / CJM (J-3 ₹1,11,000–₹1,63,030) → Senior Civil Judge (Selection Grade, J-4 ₹1,39,200–₹1,79,150) → District & Sessions Judge / ADJ (J-5 ₹1,44,840–₹1,94,660) → Principal District Judge (Super Time Scale, J-7 ₹1,99,100–₹2,24,100) → Judge, High Court of Karnataka (Constitutional Post, ₹2,25,000 fixed).
- **Exam Scheme**:
  - Stage 1: Preliminary Examination (OMR Screening, 100 marks, 120 mins): Part A Civil Law (40m), Part B Criminal Law (40m), Part C GK & Reasoning (20m). Shortlisting 1:25 for Mains.
  - Stage 2: Main Written Examination (Descriptive, 400 marks): Paper 1 Translation (100m, 180 mins); Paper 2 Law I - Civil (100m, 180 mins); Paper 3 Law II - Criminal (100m, 180 mins); Paper 4 Law III - Judgment Writing & Framing of Issues (100m, 180 mins). Qualifying: 40% per paper (35% SC/ST) and 50% aggregate (40% SC/ST).
  - Stage 3: Viva-Voce / Oral Interview (100 marks; qualifying 40% General/OBC, 35% SC/ST).
- **Compensation**: Entry Basic Pay ₹77,840 (SNJPC Level J-1). At 58% DA (₹45,147) + official judicial bungalow / HRA + sumptuary allowance + conveyance, estimated gross pay is ₹1,32,000–₹1,48,000 and in-hand pay is ₹1,16,000–₹1,29,000.
- **Competition Benchmarks**: In recent cycles, 57 to 106 vacancies attracted 13,000 to 16,000 law graduate applicants.

### 3.5 BESCOM Assistant Engineer (`bescom-assistant-engineer`)
- **Conducting Authority**: Bangalore Electricity Supply Company Limited (BESCOM) and KPTCL Joint Selection Committee (`https://bescom.karnataka.gov.in`, `https://kptcl.karnataka.gov.in`).
- **Statutory Framework**: BESCOM Cadre & Recruitment Regulations and Karnataka Power Utility Revised Pay Scales (Advt. No. KPTCL/B16/21723/2021-22).
- **Career Trajectory**: Assistant Engineer (AE Electrical / Civil, Scale ₹41,130–₹72,920) → Assistant Executive Engineer (AEE / Sub-Division Head, Scale ₹57,120–₹1,06,860) → Executive Engineer (EE / Division Head BMAZ/BRAZ, Scale ₹74,400–₹1,12,800) → Superintending Engineer (SE / Circle In-charge, Scale ₹90,500–₹1,23,300) → Chief Engineer (CE / Zonal O&M, Scale ₹1,04,600–₹1,41,300) → Director (Technical) / Managing Director.
- **Exam Scheme**:
  - Stage 1: Compulsory Kannada Language Test (150 marks, 120 mins; qualifying 35%).
  - Stage 2: Aptitude Examination (CBT/OMR, 100 marks, 120 mins, -0.25 negative marking): 75 marks Core Engineering Degree Curriculum (B.E./B.Tech Electrical & Electronics / Civil) + 25 marks General Awareness & Aptitude.
  - Stage 3: Document Verification & Medical Examination.
- **Compensation**: Entry Basic Pay ₹41,130 (Scale ₹41,130–₹72,920, Executive Officer Cadre). At 58% DA (₹23,855) + 27% HRA in Bengaluru BBMP limits (₹11,105), estimated gross pay is ₹69,000–₹78,000 and in-hand pay is ₹61,000–₹69,000.
- **Competition Benchmarks**: Combined ESCOMs recruitment drive for 533 AE vacancies attracted 1,18,400 engineering graduate applicants across Karnataka.

### 3.6 KCET (`kcet`)
- **Conducting Authority**: Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) (`https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea/`).
- **Statutory Framework**: Karnataka Selection of Candidates for Admission to Government seats in Professional Educational Institutions Rules.
- **Nature**: State-level academic entrance test for undergraduate engineering, technology, farm science, veterinary, BSc nursing, and pharmacy admissions across Karnataka universities and affiliated colleges.
- **Exam Scheme**:
  - Stage 1: Common Entrance Test (Offline OMR, 240 marks total over 2 days, 4 sessions of 80 mins and 60 marks each): Day 1 Session 1 Biology (60m), Session 2 Mathematics (60m); Day 2 Session 3 Physics (60m), Session 4 Chemistry (60m). No negative marking.
  - Stage 2: Kannada Language Test for Horanadu & Gadinadu Kannadiga Candidates (50 marks, 60 mins; qualifying 12 marks).
  - Stage 3: Centralized Online Counselling & Document Verification (50:50 composite merit computation: 50% KCET PCM marks + 50% 2nd PUC Board PCM marks).
- **Competition Benchmarks**: In recent cycles, 3,49,637 candidates registered; 3,10,314 appeared; 2,74,595 qualified for engineering merit ranks across ~1,20,000 state quota engineering and professional seats.
