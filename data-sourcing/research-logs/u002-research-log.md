## 2. Detailed Exam Dossier Records

### 2.1 `ssc-cgl` — Combined Graduate Level Examination
- **Official Sources Used**:
  - Notification: Staff Selection Commission Notice of CGLE 2024 (dated 24.06.2024)
  - Pay Scale: 7th Central Pay Commission Civilian Pay Matrix Table 5
  - Official Portal: `https://ssc.gov.in`
- **Key Findings**:
  - Recruits Group 'B' and 'C' officers across central ministries (CSS, MEA, CBDT, CBIC, CAG, CBI, ED).
  - Benchmark Level: Pay Level 7 (₹44,900 basic) for Inspectors and Assistant Section Officers.
  - Exam Pattern: Tier I (Computer Based Screening, 200 marks, qualifying) + Tier II (Paper I 390 merit marks + qualifying Computer Knowledge and DEST typing module).
  - Vacancies: 17,727 (2024), 8,415 (2023), 36,012 (2022).

### 2.2 `ssc-chsl` — Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination
- **Official Sources Used**:
  - Notification: Staff Selection Commission Notice of CHSLE 2024 (dated 08.04.2024)
  - Official Portal: `https://ssc.gov.in`
- **Key Findings**:
  - Entry clerical/data posts: LDC/JSA at Level 2 (₹19,900) and DEO at Level 4 (₹25,500).
  - Exam Pattern: Tier I (200 marks qualifying) + Tier II (360 marks merit: Maths, Reasoning, English, GA) + qualifying Computer Knowledge & Typing/Skill Test.
  - Vacancies: 3,712 (2024), 1,211 (2023), 4,522 (2022).

### 2.3 `rrb-ntpc` — RRB Non-Technical Popular Categories
- **Official Sources Used**:
  - Notification: Ministry of Railways Centralized Employment Notices CEN 05/2024 (Graduate) & CEN 06/2024 (Undergraduate)
  - Official Portal: `https://rrb.indianrailways.gov.in/chandigarh`
- **Key Findings**:
  - Recruits Station Masters (Level 6, ₹35,400), Goods Train Managers (Level 5), and Clerks (Level 2/3).
  - Multi-stage selection: CBT-1 (100 marks screening), CBT-2 (120 marks merit), and CBAT/Typing test.
  - Vacancies: 11,558 (2024), 35,281 (CEN 01/2019 with over 1.25 crore applicants).

### 2.4 `rrb-group-d` — RRB Level-1 Posts
- **Official Sources Used**:
  - Notification: Ministry of Railways Centralized Employment Notice RRC-01/2019
  - Official Portal: `https://rrb.indianrailways.gov.in/chandigarh`
- **Key Findings**:
  - Recruits Track Maintainers Grade IV, Pointsmen, and Workshop Assistants at Level 1 (₹18,000 basic).
  - Selection: Single stage CBT (100 marks, 90 mins) + Physical Efficiency Test (weight carry 35kg/20kg & 1000m run) + Medical Examination.
  - Vacancies: 1,03,769 (RRC-01/2019), 62,907 (CEN 02/2018).

### 2.5 `ssc-mts` — Multi-Tasking Staff & Havaldar
- **Official Sources Used**:
  - Notification: Staff Selection Commission Notice of MTS & Havaldar 2024 (dated 27.06.2024)
  - Official Portal: `https://ssc.gov.in`
- **Key Findings**:
  - General Central Service Group 'C' non-ministerial posts at Level 1 (₹18,000 basic).
  - Computer Based Exam: Session I (Maths/Reasoning, qualifying, no negative marking) and Session II (General Awareness & English, 150 marks merit, 1 mark negative marking). Havaldar candidates take PET walking test.
  - Vacancies: 9,583 (2024), 1,773 (2023), 12,523 (2022).

### 2.6 `ssc-gd` — Constable (GD) in CAPFs and Assam Rifles
- **Official Sources Used**:
  - Notification: Staff Selection Commission Notice of Constable (GD) 2025 (dated 05.09.2024)
  - Official Portal: `https://ssc.gov.in`
- **Key Findings**:
  - Subordinate combatised cadre across BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, SSB, SSF, and Assam Rifles at Level 3 (₹21,700 basic).
  - Selection: Computer Based Exam (80 Qs, 160 marks, 0.25 negative marking) + PST + PET (5 km race in 24 min for males) + DME.
  - Vacancies: 39,481 (2025), 46,617 (2024), 50,187 (2022).

### 2.7 `ssc-je` — Junior Engineer Examination
- **Official Sources Used**:
  - Notification: Staff Selection Commission Notice of JE 2024 (dated 28.03.2024)
  - Official Portal: `https://ssc.gov.in`
- **Key Findings**:
  - Technical engineering posts (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical) in CPWD, MES, CWC, and BRO at Level 6 (₹35,400 basic).
  - Scheme: Paper I (200 marks, non-technical + general engineering) + Paper II (300 marks technical CBT). Total merit: 500 marks.
  - Vacancies: 1,765 (2024), 1,324 (2023), 2,798 (2022).

### 2.8 `ssc-steno` — Stenographer Grade 'C' & 'D' Examination
- **Official Sources Used**:
  - Notification: Staff Selection Commission Notice of Stenographer 2024 (dated 26.07.2024)
  - Official Portal: `https://ssc.gov.in`
- **Key Findings**:
  - Secretarial staff at Level 4 (Grade D, ₹25,500) and Level 6/7 (Grade C, ₹35,400 / ₹44,900).
  - Selection: Computer Based Exam (200 marks: Reasoning 50, GA 50, English 100) + qualifying Skill Test in Stenography (80/100 wpm).
  - Vacancies: 2,006 (2024), 1,207 (2023), 3,960 (2022).
