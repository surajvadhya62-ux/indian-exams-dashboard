## 2. Selection Scheme (Exam Architecture)

### Mark Allocation for Group C posts (including LDC / Junior Stenographer):

| Component | Marks |
|---|---|
| Written Test (CBT / OMR) | 80 |
| Local Domicile Weightage* | 20 |
| **Total Merit** | **100** |

*\* Domicile Certificate issued by Mamlatdar of UT of Daman & Diu. Credited only after obtaining ≥ 33% in written test.*

### Written Examination Details:
- **Total questions:** 80 MCQs
- **Marks per question:** 1 mark
- **Negative marking:** -0.25 per wrong answer
- **Qualifying cutoff:** 33% (candidates below this threshold are not merit-listed and domicile marks are not credited)
- **Mode:** Computer Based (CBT) or OMR, as decided by Board from time to time
- **Medium:** English / Hindi (bilingual)

### Sections (4 × 20 marks):
1. General Intelligence & Reasoning (20 Qs × 1 mark)
2. General Awareness (20 Qs × 1 mark)
3. Quantitative Aptitude / Numerical Aptitude (20 Qs × 1 mark)
4. English Language & Comprehension (20 Qs × 1 mark)

### Qualifying Tests:
- **Computer Typing Test:** 35 wpm English OR 30 wpm Hindi on computer (qualifying only, not counted in merit)

### Other Provisions:
- **No interview** for Group B, C, MTS posts (Clause 2 of OM 1/1/87-CS/PF/1804)
- **Examination fee:** Rs.100/- (no exemption, non-refundable)
- **Tie-breaking:** Senior in age ranked higher; if tied, higher marks in essential educational qualification
- **Candidates with disabilities:** 20 extra minutes per hour on pro-rata basis
