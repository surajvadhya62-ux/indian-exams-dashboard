## 1. Summary of Unit Execution

Unit 45 encompasses the three premier specialized state-level recruitment and eligibility bodies of the State of Rajasthan outside RPSC and RSMSSB:
1. `reet`: Rajasthan Eligibility Examination for Teachers (BSER Ajmer / Board of Secondary Education Rajasthan) — **Tier A**
2. `rajasthan-rjs`: Rajasthan Judicial Service (Civil Judge Cadre) Examination (High Court of Judicature for Rajasthan, Jodhpur) — **Tier A**
3. `rvunl-junior-engineer`: Rajasthan Rajya Vidyut Utpadan Nigam Junior Engineer Exam (RVUNL Common Recruitment for 5 State Power Companies) — **Tier A**

All 3 examinations are classified as **Tier A**, requiring exhaustive research and full population across all five schema sections (`career_ladder`, `exam_scheme`, `financial_package`, `competition_benchmarks`, and `official_downloads`). Every single fact and number was obtained directly from primary statutory gazettes, detailed advertisements, and official result notifications opened and inspected this session:

- **REET (BSER Ajmer)**:
  - **Statutory Framework**: Governed under Section 23(1) of the Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education (RTE) Act, 2009, NCTE statutory guidelines, and Rajasthan Educational (State and Subordinate) Service Rules, 2021. Primary document opened and inspected: BSER REET 2022 Detailed Advertisement & Guidelines (`PressVighyapti2022.pdf`, 1.53 MB).
  - **Pay & Remuneration**: Grade III Teachers appointed pursuant to REET qualification are placed in **Pay Matrix Level L-10** (Entry Basic Pay **₹33,800**) under the Rajasthan Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 2017. During the mandatory two-year probation period, a fixed trainee remuneration of **₹23,700/month** is paid. Post-probation gross salary ranges from ₹55,000 to ₹62,500 (with 58% DA = ₹19,604 and 10%–20% HRA), with in-hand earnings of ₹48,000 to ₹55,500 after NPS/RGHS deductions.
  - **Exam Scheme**: Conducted as an offline OMR test in two levels: Level 1 (Classes I–V, 5 sections: Child Development & Pedagogy, Language I, Language II, Mathematics, Environmental Studies) and Level 2 (Classes VI–VIII, 4 sections: Child Development & Pedagogy, Language I, Language II, Mathematics & Science OR Social Studies). Both levels comprise **150 MCQs, 150 marks, 150 minutes duration**, with strictly **no negative marking**. Certificates carry lifetime validity (notified vide Primary Education Department order dated 16.03.2022). Minimum qualifying threshold: 60% General, 55% SC/ST/OBC/MBC/EWS, 50% Widows/Ex-Servicemen, 40% PwBD, 36% Sahariya / TSP ST.
  - **Competition Scale**: REET represents Rajasthan's largest public competitive examination. In REET 2022, 14,71,310 candidates appeared out of 15,66,992 registered applicants, and 8,07,145 candidates qualified (6,03,536 Level 2; 2,03,609 Level 1) for 48,000 Grade 3 Teacher posts advertised by the State Elementary Education Department. In REET 2021, over 16.51 lakh candidates applied for 31,000 vacancies. Historical registrations from BSER statistical compendia show 5,25,546 candidates in RTET 2012 and 6,08,576 candidates in RTET 2011.

- **Rajasthan Judicial Service (RJS — Civil Judge Cadre)**:
  - **Statutory Framework**: Governed by the Rajasthan Judicial Service Rules, 2010 (as amended) and conducted directly by the High Court of Judicature for Rajasthan at Jodhpur. Primary documents inspected: Detailed Advertisement Advt. No. RHC/Exam Cell/RJS/CJC/2024/783 dated 09.04.2024, Preliminary Result Notice No. 1851 dated 15.07.2024, Main Examination Result Notice No. 2530 dated 01.10.2024, and Final Selection Recommendation Notice No. 3249 dated 27.10.2024.
  - **Judicial Pay Scale**: Remunerated under the Second National Judicial Pay Commission (SNJPC) revised scale of **₹77,840 – ₹1,36,520** (Entry Basic Pay **₹77,840**). With 58% DA (₹45,147) and judicial allowances (Sumptuary, Robe, accommodation/HRA, electricity/water reimbursement, personal security), gross monthly earnings range from ₹1,32,000 to ₹1,48,000 (net in-hand ₹1,18,000 to ₹1,32,000).
  - **Exam Scheme**: Three-tier competitive selection:
    1. Preliminary Examination (Objective OMR): 100 MCQs, 100 marks (70% Law Paper I & II, 30% Hindi & English proficiency), 2 hours, **no negative marking**, qualifying only for Mains (15x vacancies shortlisted; qualifying cutoff: 45% Gen/OBC/EWS/MBC, 40% SC/ST/PwBD).
    2. Main Examination (Subjective Written): 4 papers totaling 300 marks (Law Paper-I 100 marks / 3 hrs; Law Paper-II 100 marks / 3 hrs; Language Hindi Essay 50 marks / 2 hrs; Language English Essay 50 marks / 2 hrs). Qualifying threshold: minimum 35% in each law paper and 40% aggregate in Mains (30% law / 35% aggregate for SC/ST/PwBD).
    3. Interview / Viva-Voce: 35 marks (3x vacancies called). Total marks for final merit = 335 marks.
  - **Competition Benchmarks**: In the 2024 cycle, 222 vacancies were advertised (83 for 2022, 57 for 2023, 82 for 2024). Approximately 45,000 law graduates appeared in Prelims on 23.06.2024. A total of 3,761 candidates were shortlisted for Mains (Prelim cutoff: Gen 73/95, OBC 68, EWS 68, SC 55, ST 54). For Mains, ~550 candidates qualified for Interview (Mains cutoff: Gen 131/300, OBC 123, EWS 126.5, SC 105, ST 105). 222 candidates were recommended on 27.10.2024.

- **RVUNL Junior Engineer-I (Rajasthan State Power Companies)**:
  - **Statutory Framework**: Centralized Common Recruitment conducted by Rajasthan Rajya Vidyut Utpadan Nigam Limited (RVUNL) on behalf of all five State Power Utilities: RVUN (Genco), RVPN (Transco), JVVN (Jaipur Discom), AVVN (Ajmer Discom), and JdVVN (Jodhpur Discom). Primary document opened and inspected: Common Recruitment Short Advt. No. RVUN/Rectt.-2026-27/01 dated 24.06.2026 and Detailed Advt. No. RVUN/Rectt.-2026-27/02 dated 04.08.2026 (`1834ef1422d-0e7f-4f51-bfd3-6c602a408063.pdf`, 131 KB text).
  - **Pay & Remuneration**: Governed under Rajasthan Vidyut Nigams Revised Pay Regulations in **Pay Matrix Level L-10** (Entry Basic Pay **₹33,800**; Grade Pay ₹3,600 equivalent). Appointees undergo two years of probation training on fixed stipend of **₹23,700/month**. Regular gross earnings post-probation range from ₹55,000 to ₹63,000 (with plant generation allowances and subsidized electricity quotas), with net in-hand earnings of ₹48,000 to ₹56,000.
  - **Exam Scheme**: Single-stage online Computer Based Test (CBT) Common Written Competitive Examination of 2 hours duration (120 minutes) comprising Part A (60% weightage, 120 marks, Degree-level discipline engineering) and Part B (40% weightage, 80 marks, Reasoning, Math Class-XII, Rajasthan GK & Everyday Science, Hindi, English). Negative marking applies (0.25 penalty per wrong answer). Strictly no interview; selection is determined entirely on CBT marks, followed by document verification (2x vacancies called).
  - **Competition Benchmarks**: In the 2026-27 cycle, 869 Junior Engineer-I vacancies (Electrical: 727, Mechanical: 110, Civil: 32) were advertised out of 2,005 total vacancies across the 5 power companies, attracting ~95,000 engineering applicants. In the 2021 cycle, 946 JEN-I posts were filled across ~1,20,000 candidates; 1,027 posts were advertised in 2018.
