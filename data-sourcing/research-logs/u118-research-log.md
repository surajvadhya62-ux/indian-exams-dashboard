## 1. Administrative Framework & Statutory Authority

The **Chhattisgarh Professional Examination Board (CG Vyapam / छत्तीसगढ़ व्यावसायिक परीक्षा मण्डल, रायपुर)** is the statutory entrance examination conducting authority established by the State Government of Chhattisgarh under the **Chhattisgarh Professional Examination Board Act, 2005 (Chhattisgarh Act No. 1 of 2005)**.

- **Headquarters:** Vyapam Bhavan, North Block, Sector-19, Atal Nagar, Nava Raipur, Chhattisgarh – 492 002
- **Official Portal:** `https://vyapam.cgstate.gov.in`
- **Application & Candidate Hub:** `https://vyapamcg.cgstate.gov.in`
- **Participating Higher Education Bodies:**
  - **Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Chhattisgarh:** Responsible for centralized online admission counselling, seat reservation, and allotment for undergraduate B.E. / B.Tech engineering programs across colleges affiliated with **Chhattisgarh Swami Vivekanand Technical University (CSVTU), Bhilai**.
  - **Indira Gandhi Krishi Vishwavidyalaya (IGKV), Raipur:** Statutory state agricultural university administering admissions and online counselling for B.Sc. (Hons) Agriculture, B.Sc. (Hons) Horticulture, B.Sc. (Hons) Forestry, and B.Tech (Agricultural Engineering / Dairy Technology).
  - **Dau Shri Vasudev Chandrakar Kamdhenu Vishwavidyalaya (DSVCKV), Durg:** State veterinary and animal sciences university regulating polytechnic diplomas in animal husbandry and fisheries science.

### Statutory Architecture for Entrance Examinations:
1. **The Chhattisgarh Professional Examination Board Act, 2005:** Empowers CG Vyapam to design syllabus schemes, administer offline/online objective screening tests, publish model answer keys, adjudicate candidate objections, evaluate OMR responses, and declare state merit lists.
2. **DTE Departmental Admission Rules for Degree Engineering (B.E./B.Tech):** Prescribes that 85% of total approved seats in government and private engineering institutes of Chhattisgarh are filled through CG PET state merit ranks (Chhattisgarh state quota), while 15% seats are reserved for JEE Main candidates (All India quota).
3. **IGKV Departmental Admission Rules for Agricultural Degrees:** Governs eligibility (10+2 with PCM, PCB, or Agriculture subjects), vertical/horizontal state reservations, domicile verification, and centralized merit counselling.
4. **Entrance Exam Exemption Rule (§RESEARCH-GUIDE §3):** As academic entrance examinations for undergraduate admissions, both exams strictly omit recruitment keys `career_ladder` and `financial_package` (keys are absent, not empty objects).
