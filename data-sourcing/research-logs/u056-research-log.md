### 1.1 Key Institutional Findings & Regulatory Context

- **HSSC CET Architecture & Group Structure**:
  - In 2022, Haryana instituted the Common Eligibility Test (CET) under the *Haryana Staff Selection Commission (Common Eligibility Test) Policy, 2022* (Notification No. 42/119/2019-5GS-I).
  - **Group C Architecture**: Operates as a two-tier mechanism. Stage 1 is the general CET Group C screening exam (100 questions, 105 minutes, 0.95 marks each). Shortlisted candidates (typically 4 times the number of vacancies in each job category) appear in Stage 2 Mains written exams organized into 63 distinct functional job groups (e.g., Group 56 for Graduate Level posts including Patwari, Canal Patwari, Gram Sachiv; Group 57 for Higher Secondary 10+2 Level posts including Ministerial Clerks; Technical Groups for engineering/health posts).
  - **Group D Architecture**: Unlike Group C, Group D selection operates on a single-stage model. Candidates take a 100-question (94-mark / 95-mark) OMR examination covering general subjects (75%) and Haryana General Knowledge (25%). Selection, department allotment, and appointment (Peon, Beldar, Mali, Chowkidar, Field Attendant) are determined directly from the CET Group D merit list without any secondary written exam.
  - **Mandatory Fifth Option**: Under HSSC examination reforms to prevent fraudulent OMR tampering, every question features five answer bubbles. If a candidate chooses not to answer a question, darkening the fifth bubble ('E' - question left unattempted) is mandatory. Failure to bubble any of the five options incurs a deduction of marks equal to the question's value (0.95 or 0.94 marks).

- **Haryana Civil Services Pay Architecture**:
  - Remuneration across all state government departments, boards, and corporations is structured under the *Haryana Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 2016* utilizing Functional Pay Levels (FPL):
    - **Group D**: FPL-DL (entry basic pay ₹16,900, scale ₹16,900 – ₹53,500).
    - **Clerk**: FPL-2 (entry basic pay ₹19,900, scale ₹19,900 – ₹63,200).
    - **Police Constable**: FPL-3 (entry basic pay ₹21,700, scale ₹21,700 – ₹69,100).
    - **Patwari / Canal Patwari / Gram Sachiv**: FPL-4 (entry basic pay ₹25,500, scale ₹25,500 – ₹81,100).
    - **Police Sub-Inspector / PRT Teacher**: FPL-6 (entry basic pay ₹35,400, scale ₹35,400 – ₹1,12,400).
    - **TGT Teacher**: FPL-7 (entry basic pay ₹44,900, scale ₹44,900 – ₹1,42,400).
    - **PGT Teacher**: FPL-8 (entry basic pay ₹47,600, scale ₹47,600 – ₹1,51,100).
    - **Assistant Engineer (Power Utilities)**: FPL-9 (entry basic pay ₹53,100, scale ₹53,100 – ₹1,67,800).
  - Dearness Allowance (DA) is pegged to the project constant of 58% effective 1 July 2025.

- **Haryana Police Recruitment Overhaul**:
  - Regulated by the *Punjab Police Rules, 1934* (as applicable to Haryana) and the *Haryana Police Act, 2007*.
  - Under Advt 01/2024 and Advt 06/2024, direct recruitment of 6,000 Constables (5,000 Male GD, 1,000 Female GD) requires candidates to possess a valid CET Group C qualifying score. Shortlisted candidates undergo Physical Screening Test (PST: 2.5 km in 12 min for male, 1.0 km in 6 min for female) and Physical Measurement Test (PMT), followed by a 94.5-mark Knowledge Test and bonus marks for NCC certificates.
  - Sub-Inspector recruitment (Advt 03/2021) features an 80-mark written test (100 questions of 0.80 marks each), qualifying PST/PMT, and up to 20 marks for higher educational qualifications and NCC certification.

- **Haryana Power Utilities (HPUs) Centralized Engineering Recruitment**:
  - The four state power entities — Haryana Vidyut Prasaran Nigam Limited (HVPNL), Uttar Haryana Bijli Vitran Nigam (UHBVNL), Dakshin Haryana Bijli Vitran Nigam (DHBVNL), and Haryana Power Generation Corporation Limited (HPGCL) — coordinate centralized recruitment of Assistant Engineers through HVPNL.
  - Selection is anchored directly on normalized marks obtained in the national Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) in Electrical, Civil, Mechanical, or Computer Science/IT disciplines, combined with verification of state domicile and educational qualifications.

- **Board of School Education Haryana (HTET)**:
  - Administered by BSEH Bhiwani under Section 23(1) of the Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act (RTE), 2009.
  - Operates across three distinct levels: Level 1 (PRT, classes 1–5), Level 2 (TGT, classes 6–8), and Level 3 (PGT, classes 9–12). Each paper carries 150 MCQs (150 minutes, 150 marks) with a 60% qualifying threshold (90 marks) for General and 55% (82 marks) for SC/differently-abled candidates of Haryana.
