## 1. Corporate Governance, Statutory Mandates & Pay Frameworks

Unit `u135` encompasses premier Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs) holding Maharatna and Miniratna Category-I status, together with the statutory airport authority of India:

### A. Energy, Hydrocarbons & Natural Gas Maharatnas
1. **GAIL (India) Limited (`gail-executive-trainee`)**:
   - Maharatna CPSE under the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas (MoPNG), Government of India.
   - Operates over 16,000 km of natural gas transmission pipeline network and petrochemical facilities.
   - Cadre: Executive Trainee (ET) in Grade E-2 (`₹60,000 – ₹1,80,000`). Inducted via GATE score followed by GD & PI.
   - Financials: DPE 3rd PRC IDA scale. Basic pay ₹60,000, canonical DA constant 58% as of 2025-07-01 (₹34,800), 35% cafeteria perks (₹21,000), HRA (9% to 27%). Gross: ₹1,15,000–₹1,36,000/month; In-Hand: ₹98,000–₹1,18,000/month. CTC ~₹18 Lakhs/year.

2. **Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited (`ongc-graduate-trainee`)**:
   - India's flagship Maharatna exploration and production company under MoPNG, responsible for ~70% of domestic crude oil and natural gas output.
   - Cadre: Graduate Trainee (GT) in Grade E-1 (`₹60,000 – ₹1,80,000`). Selected through GATE score (60 marks), academic performance (25 marks), and Personal Interview (15 marks).
   - Financials: Entry basic pay ₹60,000 at 58% IDA (₹34,800), 35% cafeteria perks (₹21,000), offshore allowances for 14-day rotational platform shifts. Gross: ₹1,15,000–₹1,42,000/month; In-Hand: ₹98,000–₹1,22,000/month. CTC ~₹20–22 Lakhs/year.

3. **Indian Oil Corporation Limited (`iocl-non-executive`)**:
   - Maharatna refining and marketing major under MoPNG operating 11 refineries and extensive pan-India pipeline infrastructure.
   - Cadre: Non-Executive Personnel (Junior Engineering Assistant-IV / Engineering Assistant) in Grade IV (`₹25,000 – ₹1,05,000`). Selection through CBT (100 MCQs, 120 mins) followed by qualifying Skill/Proficiency/Physical Test (SPPT).
   - Financials: Entry basic pay ₹25,000, 58% IDA (₹14,500), 35% cafeteria perks (₹8,750), HRA (9% to 27%). Gross: ₹50,000–₹62,000/month; In-Hand: ₹43,000–₹53,000/month. CTC ~₹7.5–8.5 Lakhs/year.

### B. Coal & Heavy Steel Manufacturing Maharatnas
4. **Coal India Limited (`cil-management-trainee`)**:
   - Maharatna enterprise under the Ministry of Coal, the world's largest coal producer operating across 8 subsidiaries (ECL, BCCL, CCL, WCL, SECL, NCL, MCL, CMPDIL).
   - Cadre: Management Trainee (MT) in Grade E-2 (`₹50,000 – ₹1,60,000`) during training, regularized into Grade E-3 (`₹60,000 – ₹1,80,000`) as Assistant Manager. Selection via CBT (Paper-I Aptitude 100 marks + Paper-II Domain 100 marks, 180 mins) or GATE score for core mining.
   - Financials: Trainee basic ₹50,000, 58% IDA (₹29,000), 35% cafeteria perks (₹17,500), underground allowance (9% to 15% for mining). Gross: ₹98,000–₹1,18,000/month (trainee); ₹1,18,000–₹1,40,000/month (absorbed).

5. **Steel Authority of India Limited (`sail-mtt`)**:
   - Maharatna steelmaking CPSE under the Ministry of Steel operating 5 integrated steel plants (Bhilai, Bokaro, Rourkela, Durgapur, IISCO Burnpur).
   - Cadre: Management Trainee (Technical) (MTT) in Grade E-1 (`₹50,000 – ₹1,60,000`). Inducted via GATE score (75% weightage), Group Discussion (10%), and Personal Interview (15%).
   - Financials: Basic pay ₹50,000, 58% IDA (₹29,000), 35% cafeteria perks (₹17,500), steel township housing and hospital facilities. Gross: ₹98,000–₹1,15,000/month; In-Hand: ₹84,000–₹1,00,000/month. CTC ~₹16 Lakhs/year.

### C. Power Generation & Heavy Engineering Maharatnas
6. **NTPC Limited (`ntpc-eet`)**:
   - India's largest power generating utility, Maharatna CPSE under the Ministry of Power with over 76 GW installed capacity.
   - Cadre: Engineering Executive Trainee (EET) in Grade E-1 (`₹40,000 – ₹1,40,000`). Selection is 100% based on normalized GATE score followed by document verification and medical fitness test (no interviews).
   - Financials: Basic pay ₹40,000, 58% IDA (₹23,200), 35% cafeteria perks (₹14,000), thermal power station allowances, township accommodation. Gross: ₹82,000–₹98,000/month; In-Hand: ₹71,000–₹85,000/month. CTC ~₹14.50 Lakhs/year.

7. **Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (`bhel-engineer-trainee`)**:
   - Maharatna engineering and manufacturing enterprise under the Ministry of Heavy Industries.
   - Cadre: Engineer Trainee (ET) in Grade E-1 (`₹50,000 – ₹1,60,000`) during training, regularized as Engineer in Grade E-1 (`₹60,000 – ₹1,80,000`). Selection through standalone CBT (240 questions / 150 mins; 75% weightage) and Personal Interview (25% weightage).
   - Financials: Trainee basic ₹50,000, 58% IDA (₹29,000), cafeteria perks up to 35% (₹17,500). Regularized basic ₹60,000, 58% IDA (₹34,800). Gross: ₹98,000–₹1,15,000/month (trainee); ₹1,18,000–₹1,38,000/month (absorbed).

### D. Civil Aviation & Air Navigation Statutory Corporation
8. **Airports Authority of India (`aai-atc-junior-executive`)**:
   - Statutory body created by the Airports Authority of India Act, 1994 under the Ministry of Civil Aviation, managing 137+ airports and national sovereign airspace.
   - Cadre: Junior Executive (Air Traffic Control) in Executive Grade E-1 (`₹40,000 – ₹1,40,000`). Selection via CBT (Part A General Aptitude 60 marks + Part B Physics & Mathematics 60 marks, 120 mins, no negative marking) followed by Voice Test, Psychoactive Substances screening, and ICAO Class 3 Medical Examination.
   - Financials: Basic pay ₹40,000, 58% IDA (₹23,200), 35% cafeteria perks (₹14,000), ATCO rating & stress allowances. Gross: ₹88,000–₹1,02,000/month; In-Hand: ₹76,000–₹88,000/month. CTC ~₹13 Lakhs/year.
