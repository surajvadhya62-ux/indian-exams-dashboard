## 1. Administrative Framework & Strategic Recruiting Authorities

Unit `u127` covers premier national defence, internal security, and strategic technical direct entry positions under the **Ministry of Defence (MoD)** and **Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA)**:

### A. Armed Forces & Agnipath Scheme (Indian Army)
- **Recruiting Authority**: Directorate General of Recruiting, Integrated Headquarters of Ministry of Defence (Army), New Delhi.
- **Official Portal**: `https://joinindianarmy.nic.in`
- **Cadre Structure**: Enlistment under the Agnipath Scheme for a fixed 4-year term with up to 25% of each batch selected for absorption into the permanent regular cadre as Sepoy / Rifleman / Gunner / Sowar (Pay Matrix Level 3: ₹21,700 – ₹69,100 + ₹5,200 MSP).
- **Remuneration Model**: Year 1 to 4 fixed emoluments (₹30,000 to ₹40,000 per month) with 30% contributed to the Agniveer Corpus Fund, matched equally by GOI, culminating in a tax-free Seva Nidhi exit package of ~₹11.71 Lakhs.

### B. Intelligence Bureau (Ministry of Home Affairs)
- **Recruiting Authority**: Joint Deputy Director (Executive) / Intelligence Bureau, Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India.
- **Official Portal**: `https://www.mha.gov.in`
- **Cadre Structure**: ACIO-II/Exe is the foundational direct-entry executive intelligence officer cadre (Central Civil Services Group 'C' Non-Gazetted / Non-Ministerial, Pay Level 7: ₹44,900 – ₹1,42,400).
- **Special Emoluments**: 20% Special Security Allowance (SSA) over basic pay without ceiling, plus cash compensation in lieu of holiday/round-the-clock duty up to 30 days per year.

### C. Indian Coast Guard (Ministry of Defence)
- **Recruiting Authority**: Directorate of Recruitment, Indian Coast Guard Headquarters, New Delhi (Administered in partnership with C-DAC).
- **Official Portal**: `https://joinindiancoastguard.cdac.in`
- **Cadres Covered**:
  - `icg-assistant-commandant`: Direct-entry Gazetted Officer (Group 'A', Pay Level 10: ₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500) via CGCAT, PSB, and FSB.
  - `icg-yantrik`: Technical warrant sailor rating (Pay Level 5: ₹29,200 – ₹92,300 + ₹6,200 Yantrik Pay) for engineering diploma holders.
  - `icg-navik-gd`: Primary general duty maritime sailor rating (Pay Level 3: ₹21,700 – ₹69,100) via CGEPT.

### D. Defence Research & Development Organisation (DRDO CEPTAM)
- **Recruiting Authority**: Centre for Personnel Talent Management (CEPTAM), DRDO, Ministry of Defence, Metcalfe House, Delhi.
- **Official Portal**: `https://www.drdo.gov.in` / `https://drdo.res.in/ceptam_noticeboard/`
- **Cadres Covered**:
  - `drdo-ceptam-sta-b`: Senior Technical Assistant 'B' (Group 'B' Non-Gazetted, Level 6: ₹35,400 – ₹1,12,400) under the Defence Research Technical Cadre (DRTC). Merit Promotion Scheme provides progressive elevation to Technical Officer ranks up to Level 12.
  - `drdo-ceptam-tech-a`: Technician 'A' (Group 'C' Non-Gazetted Industrial, Level 2: ₹19,900 – ₹63,200) for Class 10 + ITI certificate holders across 20+ engineering and workshop trades.

### E. Indian Navy Civilian Personnel (Ministry of Defence)
- **Recruiting Authority**: Directorate of Civilian Personnel, Integrated Headquarters, Ministry of Defence (Navy), New Delhi.
- **Official Portal**: `https://www.joinindiannavy.gov.in`
- **Cadre Covered**:
  - `indian-navy-incet-tradesman`: Tradesman Mate (General Central Service Group 'C' Non-Gazetted, Industrial, Pay Level 1: ₹18,000 – ₹56,900) deployed in Naval Dockyards (Mumbai, Visakhapatnam), repair yards (Kochi, Karwar), and armament depots under Eastern, Western, and Southern Naval Commands. Entitled to Factories Act Overtime Allowance (OTA) and Productivity Linked Bonus (PLB).
