## 1. Administrative Framework & Conducting Body

The **State Level Police Recruitment Board, Andhra Pradesh (SLPRB AP)** is the statutory recruiting authority responsible for direct recruitment of non-gazetted executive personnel across the Andhra Pradesh Police, including Police Constables (Civil & APSP), Sub-Inspectors (Civil & APSP), Reserve Sub-Inspectors, and allied prosecution / technical cadres.

- **Headquarters:** DGP Office Complex, Mangalagiri, Guntur District, Andhra Pradesh – 522 503
- **Chairman (2022–2024 cycles):** Shri Atul Singh, IPS (Chairman, SLPRB AP)
- **Primary Official Domain:** `https://slprb.ap.gov.in`
- **Recruitments Hub:** `https://slprb.ap.gov.in/UI/Recruitments`
- **Press Releases Hub:** `https://slprb.ap.gov.in/UI/pressRelease`
- **Document CDN / Storage Path:** `https://slprb.ap.gov.in/PDFS/`

### Statutory Instruments & Rules Governing Recruitment:
1. **Andhra Pradesh Police (Stipendiary Cadet Trainee) Rules:** Notified vide G.O.Ms.No.315 Home (Pol.C) Department, dated 13-10-1999 and subsequent amendments.
2. **Andhra Pradesh Police Subordinate Service Rules:** Notified vide G.O.Ms.No.126 Home (Police-C) Department.
3. **Government Requisition Order:** G.O.Ms.No.153 Home (Services.IV) Department dated 20-10-2022 sanctioning 6,100 Police Constable vacancies and 417 Sub-Inspector vacancies.
4. **Presidential Order on Local Reservation:** The Andhra Pradesh Public Employment (Organization of Local Cadres and Regulation of Direct Recruitment) Order, 1975 read with G.O.(P) No.763 GAD dated 15-11-1975 and G.O.Ms.No.124 GAD dated 07-03-2002 (30% non-local/local combined merit, 70% local for SI; 20% non-local/local, 80% local for Civil PC).
5. **Pay Revision Scheme:** Andhra Pradesh Revised Pay Scales 2022 (AP RPS 2022 / 11th PRC) notified vide G.O.Ms.No.1, Finance (PC-TA) Department, dated 17-01-2022.

  - Stipendiary Cadet Trainee (SCT) Police Constable (APSP) (Men) — Post Code 23 (2,520 vacancies)
  - Total Notified Vacancies: 6,100
- **Selection Architecture:**
  - **Stage 1 (PWT):** Preliminary Written Test of 200 objective MCQs (3 hours, 200 marks). Minimum qualifying: 40% for OC/EWS (80 marks), 35% for BC (70 marks), 30% for SC/ST/Ex-Servicemen (60 marks). Strictly qualifying screening test; marks not counted in final merit.
  - **Stage 2 (PMT/PET):**
    - PMT: Men height ≥ 167.6 cm, chest ≥ 86.3 cm (+5 cm expansion); Women height ≥ 152.5 cm, weight ≥ 40 kg (relaxed for agency STs).
    - PET: For Civil PC (Post Code 21), 1600m run + 1 event (100m run or long jump) qualifying in nature. For APSP PC (Post Code 23), all 3 events scored out of 100 marks (1600m 40 marks, 100m 30 marks, Long Jump 30 marks) and counted in final merit.
  - **Stage 3 (FWE):** Final Written Examination of 200 objective questions (3 hours). Civil PC graded out of 200 marks (determines 100% final merit). APSP PC graded out of 100 marks (combined with 100 marks of PET for 200 marks total merit).
- **Pay Scale (State Jurisdiction Rule §5.3):**
  - Appointed into regular Andhra Pradesh Revised Pay Scales 2022 (AP RPS 2022) Scale: **₹25,220 – ₹80,910**.
  - Entry Basic Pay: **₹25,220**.
  - Central 7th CPC is **NOT** cited.
  - Standard project benchmark DA: 58% as of 2025-07-01 (₹14,628).
  - Estimated Gross Range: ₹42,000 – ₹48,000 (reflecting rural 8% HRA vs urban 16% HRA in cities like Visakhapatnam/Vijayawada plus police allowances).
  - Estimated In-Hand Range: ₹37,000 – ₹43,000 (after 10% CPS/APGPS deduction of ₹3,985, APGLI, GIS, and PT).

### Cadre, Selection Scheme & Pay Details
- **Posts Included:**
  - Stipendiary Cadet Trainee (SCT) Sub-Inspector of Police (Civil) (Men & Women) — Post Code 11 (315 vacancies across Zones I to IV)
  - Stipendiary Cadet Trainee (SCT) Reserve Sub-Inspector of Police (APSP) (Men) — Post Code 13 (96 vacancies across 4 IR battalions)
  - Total Notified Vacancies: 417
- **Selection Architecture:**
  - **Stage 1 (PWT):** Preliminary Written Test in 2 papers (each 3 hours, 100 marks):
    - Paper I: Arithmetic & Reasoning (100 Qs, 100 marks, objective)
    - Paper II: General Studies (100 Qs, 100 marks, objective)
    - Qualifying thresholds: 40% for OC/EWS (40 marks), 35% for BC (35 marks), 30% for SC/ST/Ex-Servicemen (30 marks) in EACH paper individually. Marks not counted in final merit.
  - **Stage 2 (PMT/PET):**
    - PMT: Men height ≥ 167.6 cm, chest ≥ 86.3 cm; Women height ≥ 152.5 cm, weight ≥ 40 kg.
    - PET: For Civil SI (Post Code 11), 1600m run + 1 event qualifying in nature. For APSP RSI (Post Code 13), all 3 events scored out of 100 marks (1600m 40m, 100m 30m, Long Jump 30m) and counted in final merit.
  - **Stage 3 (FWE):** Final Written Examination in 4 papers (each 3 hours duration):
    - Paper I: English (Descriptive, Degree Standard, 100 marks) — strictly qualifying
    - Paper II: Telugu or Urdu (Descriptive, Degree Standard, 100 marks) — strictly qualifying
    - Paper III: Arithmetic & Reasoning (Objective, 200 Qs) — 200 marks for Civil SI; scaled to 100 marks for APSP RSI
    - Paper IV: General Studies (Objective, 200 Qs) — 200 marks for Civil SI; scaled to 100 marks for APSP RSI
    - For Civil SI (Post Code 11), final selection is based on 400 marks from Paper III + Paper IV.
    - For APSP RSI (Post Code 13), final selection is based on 300 marks (Paper III 100 + Paper IV 100 + PET 100).
- **Pay Scale (State Jurisdiction Rule §5.3):**
  - Appointed into regular Andhra Pradesh Revised Pay Scales 2022 (AP RPS 2022) Scale: **₹44,570 – ₹1,27,480**.
  - Entry Basic Pay: **₹44,570**.
  - Central 7th CPC is **NOT** cited.
  - Standard project benchmark DA: 58% as of 2025-07-01 (₹25,851).
  - Estimated Gross Range: ₹76,000 – ₹85,000 (reflecting rural 8% HRA vs urban 16% HRA plus investigation/thana/conveyance allowances).
  - Estimated In-Hand Range: ₹65,000 – ₹73,000 (after 10% CPS/APGPS deduction of ₹7,042, APGLI, GIS, PT, and tax withholding).

## 5. Confidence Downgrades & Audit Notes

1. **State Pay Scale Integrity (§5.3):**  
   Both exams strictly cite the Andhra Pradesh Revised Pay Scales 2022 (AP RPS 2022 / 11th PRC) as notified under G.O.Ms.No.1 Finance Department dated 17-01-2022 and specified in paragraph 30 of each respective notification. No central 7th CPC matrix or levels are referenced.
2. **Promotional Hierarchy Steps:**  
   Entry levels (Constable at ₹25,220 and Sub-Inspector at ₹44,570) are marked `verified` as they were read directly from primary notifications. Subsequent promotional levels (Head Constable, ASI, Circle Inspector, DSP, Addl SP) are marked `reported` under the AP Police Subordinate Service Rules and State Police Service Rules as their scales are derived from the official AP PRC Master Scales rather than a single direct table inside the recruitment notification.
3. **2018 Competition Benchmark Data:**  
   The 2018 statistics are designated as `reported` because SLPRB AP's current online portal serves archive notices starting from the 2022 mega-recruitment cycle, though the 2018 figures are documented from contemporaneous recruitment result releases. The 2022–2023 recruitment cycle statistics are 100% `verified` with direct quotes from SLPRB's signed press releases.
4. **URL Link Optimization:**  
   The `.aspx` endpoints for `Recruitments.aspx` and `pressRelease.aspx` return HTTP 301 redirects to the canonical extensionless paths `/UI/Recruitments` and `/UI/pressRelease`. The canonical extensionless URLs were used in `official_downloads` to guarantee clean HTTP 200 responses without redirect hops.
