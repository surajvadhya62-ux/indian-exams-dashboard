### 1.2 Examination Scheme & Architecture
- **Stage 1: Objective Entrance Examination (OMR-Based Single Sitting)**:
  - Mode: Offline pen-and-paper OMR examination.
  - Duration: 3 hours (180 minutes), typically 10:00 AM to 01:00 PM.
  - Structure: Composite question paper of 180 objective multiple-choice questions (MCQs):
    - Physics: 60 questions (60 marks)
    - Chemistry: 60 questions (60 marks)
    - Mathematics: 60 questions (60 marks)
  - Marking Scheme: +1 mark for each correct answer; negative marking of -0.25 (1/4th mark deduction) for each incorrect answer; unattempted questions receive 0 marks.
  - Test Centres: Conducted simultaneously across 7 centres in J&K and Ladakh: Jammu, Srinagar, Doda, Rajouri, Anantnag, Baramulla, and Leh.
- **Stage 2: Centralized Counseling & Seat Allotment**:
  - Centralized online preference/choice filling on `www.jkbopee.gov.in` (counseling fee ₹1,000).
  - Multi-round seat allotment (Round 1, Round 2, Round 3/Mop-Up, and Special Stray Rounds).
  - Allotment strictly based on inter-se merit rank in CET and reservation quotas under S.O. 176 of 2024 / S.O. 305.
  - Minimum Qualifying Criterion: 10+2 passing marks in PCM (minimum 45% aggregate for Open Merit / EWS, 40% for Reserved Categories SC/ST-I/ST-II/RBA/ALC/IB/OBC); Domicile of UT of J&K or Ladakh.
- **Entrance Exam Standard**: `career_ladder` and `financial_package` sections are completely omitted per Project Execution Plan §5.4.

### Notes on Session Findings & Technical Nuances
- **HEAD vs GET Request Behavior**: The JKBOPEE ASP.NET / IIS web server returns `HTTP 404 Not Found` for `HEAD` requests (`curl -sI`). However, issuing standard `GET` requests (`curl -sL`) returns `HTTP 200 OK`.
- **Referer Requirement for Downloader.ashx**: The PDF downloader endpoint `Pdf/Downloader.ashx` validates the HTTP Referer header (`-e "https://www.jkbopee.gov.in/Notifications.aspx"`) to deliver binary document streams; requests without referer return a 0-byte stream with a session cookie header.
