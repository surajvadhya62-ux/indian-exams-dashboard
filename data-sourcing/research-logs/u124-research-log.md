## 1. Administrative Framework & Statutory Authorities

Unit 124 covers major premier national entrance tests, central teacher qualification standards, central social security recruitment, and elite postgraduate quantitative finance and economics admissions:

### A. National Testing Agency (NTA) & Ministry of Education
- **Statutory Foundation**: Established in 2017 by the Ministry of Education (MoE), Government of India, as an autonomous, self-sustained premier testing organization registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860.
- **Official Domains**: `https://nta.ac.in`, `https://jeemain.nta.nic.in`, `https://neet.nta.nic.in`, `https://ugcnet.nta.ac.in`, `https://cuet.nta.nic.in`.
- **Mandate**: Entrusted with administering nationwide standardized admissions and recruitment testing including JEE (Main) for Engineering (NITs/IIITs/CFTIs), NEET (UG) for Medical (NMC/DCI), UGC-NET for Junior Research Fellowship and Assistant Professorship (UGC), CUET (UG) for central university admissions, and recruitment tests for statutory bodies (EPFO SSA).

### B. Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE)
- **Statutory Foundation**: Autonomous organization under the Ministry of Education, Government of India.
- **Official Domain**: `https://ctet.nic.in`.
- **Statutory Authority**: Administers the Central Teacher Eligibility Test (CTET) under Section 23(1) of the Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education (RTE) Act, 2009 and the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE) notifications dated 23.08.2010 and 29.07.2011.

### C. Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO)
- **Statutory Foundation**: Premier statutory social security organization governed by the Central Board of Trustees, EPFO, under the administrative superintendence of the Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India.
- **Official Domain**: `https://www.epfindia.gov.in` (`https://www.epfo.gov.in`).
- **Statutory Authority**: The Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 (EPF & MP Act, 1952).

### D. Jamnalal Bajaj Institute of Management Studies (JBIMS) & University of Mumbai
- **Statutory Foundation**: Established in 1965 by the University of Mumbai in collaboration with the Stanford Graduate School of Business. Autonomous department of the University of Mumbai.
- **Official Domain**: `https://jbims.edu`.
- **Mandate**: Master of Science in Finance (M.Sc. Finance / M.Fin.) launched on the recommendation of former Prime Minister Dr. Manmohan Singh to train elite quantitative finance professionals, structured on London School of Economics benchmarks.

### E. Indira Gandhi Institute of Development Research (IGIDR)
- **Statutory Foundation**: Advanced research institute established by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) in 1987 on its golden jubilee. Conferred autonomous Deemed to be University status under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956.
- **Official Domain**: `http://www.igidr.ac.in`.
- **Mandate**: Flagship 2-year Master of Science in Economics (M.Sc. Economics) and Ph.D. in Development Studies, recognized internationally for quantitative economic policy research.

- **Assigned Tier**: A (Tier A: all applicable sections required)

- **Exam Architecture**:
  - Mode: Computer Based Test (CBT) across two annual sessions (January and April) in 13 languages.
  - Paper 1 Structure: 3 subjects (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry). Each subject consists of 25 compulsory questions (Section A: 20 MCQs + Section B: 5 numerical value questions).
  - Total Questions & Marks: 75 questions, 300 marks. Duration: 180 minutes (3 hours).
  - Marking Scheme: +4 for correct, -1 for incorrect (applies to both Section A and Section B); 0 for unanswered.
  - Merit & Qualifying: Normalized NTA Percentile Score computed across shifts. Best of Session 1 and Session 2 NTA scores used for final All-India Rank. Top ~2.5 lakh candidates qualify to sit for JEE (Advanced) for IIT admissions.
- **Competition Benchmarks**:
