## 1. Summary of Unit Execution

Unit 85 covers the premier combined subordinate ministerial and field cadre recruitment examination in the Union Territory of Andaman & Nicobar Islands:

1. `andaman-administration-cgl`: Andaman & Nicobar Administration Combined Higher Secondary & Graduate Staff Exam (CHSLRE / CGL) — **Tier C (Job)**

### Statutory Context & Pay Architecture:
- **Union Territory Central Civil Services Cadre**:
  - The Andaman and Nicobar Islands is a Union Territory directly administered by the Central Government through the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA).
  - Staff serving in the Secretariat, island district commissionerates, and line departments are Central Government civil servants governed by the Central Civil Services (CCS) rules and the **Central 7th Central Pay Commission (CPC) Pay Matrix**.
  - **Foundational Clerical Posts**: Lower Grade Clerk (LGC) / Lower Division Clerk (LDC) are placed in **Pay Matrix Level 2** (entry basic pay ₹19,900; scale ₹19,900 – ₹63,200).
  - **Revenue & Field Posts**: Patwari and Village Level Worker (VLW) are placed in **Pay Matrix Level 3** (entry basic pay ₹21,700; scale ₹21,700 – ₹69,100).
  - **Stenographers & Higher Clerks**: Stenographer Grade-III and Higher Grade Clerk (HGC) / UDC are placed in **Pay Matrix Level 4** (entry basic pay ₹25,500; scale ₹25,500 – ₹81,100).
- **Special Island Allowances & Island Special Duty Allowance (ISDA)**:
  - Governed under Ministry of Finance orders for Central Government employees posted in the Andaman and Nicobar Islands.
  - Entitled to **Island Special Duty Allowance (ISDA)** ranging from **10%** of basic pay (Port Blair / South Andaman district) to **20%** of basic pay (remote and difficult Nicobar group of islands).
  - Standard Central DA constant of **58%** as of `2025-07-01` (`da_percent_as_of_review: 58`, `da_as_of: "2025-07-01"`).
  - Island Leave Travel Concession (LTC) entitlements providing sea/air passage between mainland ports (Kolkata/Chennai) and Port Blair.
- **Selection Architecture**:
  - Conducted directly by the Recruitment & Examination (R&E) Cell of the Secretariat, Andaman & Nicobar Administration, utilizing online Tablet-Based Testing (TBT) / Computer-Based Testing (CBT) followed by mandatory trade/skill tests (typing at 35 wpm English / 30 wpm Hindi for LDCs, shorthand at 80 wpm for Stenographers).

- **Sections Populated**: `career_ladder`, `exam_scheme`, `financial_package`, `competition_benchmarks`, `official_downloads`.
- **Career Ladder**:
  - Clerical Stream: Lower Grade Clerk / LDC (Level 2: ₹19,900 – ₹63,200) → Higher Grade Clerk / UDC (Level 4: ₹25,500 – ₹81,100) → Assistant Section Officer / Head Clerk (Level 6: ₹35,400 – ₹1,12,400) → Section Officer / Office Superintendent (Level 7: ₹44,900 – ₹1,42,400) → Assistant Secretary / Under Secretary (Level 10: ₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500).
  - Revenue Stream: Patwari (Level 3: ₹21,700 – ₹69,100) → Revenue Inspector / Kanungo (Level 5: ₹29,200 – ₹92,300) → Tehsildar / Naib Tehsildar (Level 6/7: ₹35,400 – ₹1,42,400).
- **Exam Pattern**:
  - **Stage 1**: Online Tablet Based Test (TBT) / Computer Based Test of 200 objective questions for 200 marks lasting 120 minutes (2 hours): General English (50 marks), General Intelligence & Reasoning (50 marks), Quantitative Aptitude (50 marks), General Awareness (50 marks). Negative marking of 0.25 marks per incorrect answer.
  - **Stage 2**: Qualifying Skill / Trade Test:
    - Lower Grade Clerk / Typist: Computer typing test at 35 w.p.m. in English (10,500 KDPH) or 30 w.p.m. in Hindi (9,000 KDPH) for 10 minutes duration.
    - Stenographer: Shorthand test at 80 w.p.m. and typing test at 40/45 w.p.m.
    - Forester / Field posts: Physical measurement standards (PST) and endurance test (PET).
- **Financial Package**:
  - Governed by Central 7th CPC matrix.
  - Entry Post: Lower Grade Clerk (LGC) / Lower Division Clerk (LDC).
  - Pay Matrix: **Level 2** (₹19,900 – ₹63,200). Entry Basic Pay: ₹19,900.
  - Central DA: 58% as of 2025-07-01 (₹11,542).
  - Island Special Duty Allowance (ISDA): 10% (₹1,990) to 20% (₹3,980) based on island zone.
  - Transport Allowance: ₹1,350/₹900 + DA (₹2,133/₹1,422).
  - House Rent Allowance (HRA): 9% (₹1,791) or government quarters.
  - Estimated Gross Pay: ₹36,500 – ₹42,000 / month.
  - Estimated In-Hand Pay: ₹32,000 – ₹37,000 / month (after NPS 10% deduction of ₹3,144 and insurance).
