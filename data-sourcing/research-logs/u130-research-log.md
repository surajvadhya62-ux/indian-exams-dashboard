## 1. Statutory Authorities & Research Institutions

Unit `u130` covers examinations and selection mechanisms administered by India's apex autonomous scientific, technological, and medical institutions:

### A. Department of Atomic Energy (DAE) & Allied Research Units
- **Bhabha Atomic Research Centre (`barc-oces`, `barc-stipendiary-trainee`)**:
  - `barc-oces`: Premier gateway for inducting engineering graduates and science post-graduates as Scientific Officers (Group A Gazetted) into DAE facilities (BARC, IGCAR, RRCAT, VECC, AMDER, HWB, NFC, BRIT). 1-year intensive training with ₹55,000 monthly stipend, followed by absorption at 7th CPC Level 10 (Entry Basic ₹56,100).
  - `barc-stipendiary-trainee`: Direct recruitment of diploma engineers (Category-I) and ITI/technicians (Category-II). 2-year stipend (₹24,000/₹26,000 for Cat-I), absorbed into Level 7 (Basic ₹44,900) as Scientific Assistant / C.
- **Nuclear Power Corporation of India Limited (`npcil-st`)**:
  - Central Public Sector Enterprise under DAE operating India's nuclear fleet. Cat-I trainees receive 2-year stipend (₹24,000/₹26,000) and are absorbed as Scientific Assistant / B at Level 6 (Basic ₹35,400).
- **National Board for Higher Mathematics (`nbhm-phd`)**:
  - Apex mathematics development body of DAE. Conducts nationwide PhD scholarship screening for award of JRF/SRF fellowships and admissions at IMSc, HRI, NISER, and IISERs.
- **NISER Bhubaneswar & UM-DAE CEBS Mumbai (`nest`)**:
  - National Entrance Screening Test for 5-year Integrated M.Sc. in basic sciences. All admitted students receive DAE DISHA scholarship (₹60,000/yr + ₹20,000/yr mentorship).

### B. Department of Space (DOS) / ISRO
- **ISRO Centralised Recruitment Board (`isro-technical-assistant`)**:
  - Recruits diploma engineers as Technical Assistants (Group B Non-Gazetted) across URSC, VSSC, SDSC SHAR, LPSC, SAC, NRSC, and IPRU under 7th CPC Pay Level 7 (Entry Basic ₹44,900). Selection based on Written CBT score with practical Skill Test qualifying (60% cut-off).

### C. Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)
- **Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (`cdac-project-engineer`)**:
  - Premier scientific society under MeitY executing national initiatives (National Supercomputing Mission, Microprocessor Development Programme). Recruits Project Engineers on consolidated scales equivalent to 7th CPC Level 10.

### D. Ministry of Education & Autonomous Premier Institutes
- **AIIMS New Delhi (`ini-cet`)**:
  - Central gateway for MD/MS/M.Ch (6-year)/DM (6-year)/MDS across Institutes of National Importance (AIIMS, JIPMER, NIMHANS, PGIMER, SCTIMST). 200 MCQs, 180 minutes, -0.33 negative marking.
- **IISc Bangalore (`iisc-ug`)**:
  - India's top research university admitting undergraduate students to B.Sc. (Research) and B.Tech (Math & Computing) via JEE Advanced, JEE Main, NEET-UG, and IAT.
- **IIT Bombay Design Exams (`uceed`, `ceed`)**:
  - `uceed`: B.Des admission gateway for IIT Bombay, Delhi, Guwahati, Hyderabad, and IIITDM Jabalpur (Part A CBT 200 marks + Part B Drawing 100 marks).
  - `ceed`: M.Des / Ph.D entrance for 8 IITs and IISc (Part A CBT screening + Part B Subjective design paper).
- **Organizing IITs (`jam`)**:
  - Joint Admission Test for Masters across 21 IITs and IISc. 60 questions, 100 marks (Section A MCQs, Section B MSQs, Section C NATs) across 7 science disciplines.
- **Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (`tifr-gs`)**:
  - Deemed university under DAE administering nationwide graduate school admissions across Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad.
- **JEST Committee (`jest`)**:
  - Joint Entrance Screening Test recognized as NET by SERB across 32 premier national research institutes.
- **Indian Statistical Institute (`isi-admission`)**:
  - Flagship B.Stat (Hons) Kolkata and B.Math (Hons) Bengaluru admissions via single-day two-part test (Forenoon UGA MCQ + Afternoon UGB descriptive proofs).
- **IISER Joint Admissions Committee (`iiser-aptitude`)**:
  - Computer Based Test for BS-MS admissions across 7 IISERs, IISc, and IIT Madras (60 questions across Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics, Physics, 240 marks).
- **DST & Historical Programs (`kvpy`)**:
  - Landmark KVPY scheme subsumed into DST INSPIRE from 2022-23; existing fellows continue to draw monthly fellowships.
- **HBCSE / IAPT / NBHM Olympiads (`olympiad-science`, `imo`)**:
  - Multi-tier national Olympiad pathways (NSE -> INO -> OCSC -> PDT -> International Olympiads) for science and mathematical olympiads (IOQM -> RMO -> INMO -> IMOTC -> IMO).
- **Indian Institutes of Management (`iim-cat-fellowship`)**:
  - Full-time doctoral programmes in management (FPM/Ph.D.) offering 100% tuition waivers and comprehensive research stipends (₹35,000–₹45,000/month).
