## 2. Detailed Exam Dossier Records

### 2.1 `cds` — Combined Defence Services Examination
- **Official Sources Used**:
  - Notification: `https://www.upsc.gov.in/sites/default/files/Notif-CDSI-2026-Eng-10122025.pdf` (Notice No. 02/2026.CDS-I dated 10.12.2025)
  - Pay Rules: Special Army Instructions / MoD 7th CPC Gazette for Armed Forces Officers (Table 1: Defence Pay Matrix)
  - PYQ Archive: `https://www.upsc.gov.in/examinations/previous-question-papers?field_exam_name_value=Combined+Defence+Services`
- **Key Findings**:
  - Entry rank: Lieutenant / Sub Lieutenant / Flying Officer (Level 10, ₹56,100 basic + ₹15,500 Military Service Pay).
  - Pre-commission training stipend: ₹56,100/month during IMA/INA/AFA/OTA training.
  - Written testing: 3 papers for IMA/INA/AFA (English, GK, Elementary Maths; 100 marks each, 300 marks total; 1/3 negative marking). For OTA: English and GK (200 marks total). Followed by 5-day SSB interview (300 marks for IMA/INA/AFA; 200 marks for OTA).
- **Justification of Estimates**: Gross and in-hand pay ranges reflect MSP addition and variable Field Area Allowances.

### 2.2 `ies-ese` — Engineering Services Examination
- **Official Sources Used**:
  - Notification: `https://www.upsc.gov.in/sites/default/files/Exam_Notice_ESE_2025_Eng_18092024.pdf` (Notice No. 01/2025-ENGG dated 18.09.2024)
  - Addendum: Railway Management Service (IRMS) technical posts integration addendum dated 18.10.2024
  - Pay Scale: 7th CPC Civilian Pay Matrix (Level 10, ₹56,100 entry basic)
- **Key Findings**:
  - Recruits Assistant Executive Engineers across Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, and Electronics & Telecom Engineering disciplines.
  - 3-Stage Process: Preliminary (Objective 500 marks), Mains (Conventional 600 marks), Personality Test (200 marks). Total aggregate: 1300 marks.
  - Vacancy: 434 posts (232 initial + 202 IRMS technical posts).

### 2.3 `upsc-ifs` — Indian Forest Service Examination
- **Official Sources Used**:
  - Notification: `https://www.upsc.gov.in/sites/default/files/Notice-IFSP-2026-Engl-11022026.pdf` (Notice No. 06/2026-IFS dated 11.02.2026)

- **Key Findings**:
  - Premier All India Service alongside IAS and IPS.
  - Preliminary screening via Civil Services (Preliminary) Examination (CSAT qualifying 33%).
  - Main Examination: 6 conventional papers (1400 marks) + Personality Test (300 marks) = 1700 marks total.
  - Vacancies: Approximately 120 posts in 2026 cycle.

### 2.4 `capf-ac` — Central Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants)
- **Official Sources Used**:
  - Notification: `https://www.upsc.gov.in/sites/default/files/Notif-CAPF-2024-engl-240424.pdf` (Notice No. 09/2024-CPF dated 24.04.2024)
  - Ministry of Home Affairs CAPF Service Rules
- **Key Findings**:
  - Recruits Assistant Commandants (Group A Gazetted, Level 10, ₹56,100) across BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, and SSB.
  - Multi-tier selection: Written (Paper I 250 marks objective, Paper II 200 marks descriptive), Physical Standards/Efficiency Test (PST/PET, qualifying), and Interview/Personality Test (150 marks). Final merit: 600 marks.
  - Vacancies: 506 posts in 2024 cycle.

  - Direct Recruitment Test: Recruitment Test (RT) 300 marks (75% weightage) + Interview 100 marks (25% weightage).

### 2.6 `upsc-cms` — Combined Medical Services Examination
- **Official Sources Used**:
