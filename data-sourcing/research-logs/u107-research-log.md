### Statutory & Administrative Framework:
- **Recruiting Authority & Corporate Structure**:
  - Punjab State Power Corporation Limited (PSPCL) was incorporated in 2010 pursuant to the unbundling of the erstwhile Punjab State Electricity Board (PSEB) under the Electricity Act, 2003 and the Punjab Power Sector Reform Scheme.
  - Headquartered at PSEB Head Office, The Mall, Patiala (Punjab), PSPCL operates thermal generation plants, hydel stations, and the state-wide 11kV/33kV/66kV distribution and substation infrastructure.
  - Recruits are governed by PSPCL Employees Service Regulations, Financial Circulars issued by the Corporation, and general Punjab Government statutory service rules.
- **Mandatory Punjabi Language Prerequisite**:
  - Pursuant to Government of Punjab Notification No. G.S.R.72/Const/Art.309/Amd.(22)/2022 dated 28.10.2022 amending the Punjab Civil Services (General and Common Conditions of Service) Rules, 1994, no person can be appointed to any Group 'C' post unless they possess knowledge of Punjabi language of at least Matriculation standard and pass a mandatory qualifying Punjabi language test scoring at least 50% marks.
- **Punjab Pay Structure & 3-Year Fixed Pay Probation Policy**:
  - In accordance with Government of Punjab, Department of Finance (Finance Personnel 1 Branch) Letter No. 7/42/2020-5FP1/741-746 dated 17.07.2020, newly appointed direct recruits in Punjab Government departments and statutory corporations serve a 3-year probation during which they receive only the fixed "minimum admissible pay" (entry basic pay) without DA, HRA, annual increments, or allowances (except TA and medical reimbursement).
  - Following successful completion of probation, regular allowances under the 7th CPC / Punjab Pay Commission Matrix (as adopted vide PSPCL Financial Circular No. 21/2022 dated 17.08.2022) are restored, including Dearness Allowance (standardized at the canonical constant of **58%** as of `2025-07-01`), House Rent Allowance (8% to 16% based on city classification), and professional allowances.

## 3. Detailed Exam Research Log

### 3.1 PSPCL Assistant Lineman (ALM) Exam (`pspcl-assistant-lineman`)
- **File**: `public/exam-details/pspcl-assistant-lineman.json`
- **Conducting Body**: Punjab State Power Corporation Limited (PSPCL), PSEB Head Office, The Mall, Patiala (`https://pspcl.in`, `https://billpayment.pspcl.in`)
- **Cadre**: Group 'C' Technical Field Cadre (Distribution lines, overhead pole maintenance, transformer substation support, and grid fault rectification)
- **Minimum Qualifications**:
  - Matriculation (10th Pass) or equivalent.
  - National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) in Lineman Trade.
  - Knowledge of Punjabi of at least Matriculation or equivalent level.
- **Pay Scale & Remuneration**:
  - Placed in **PSPCL Board Pay Scale / 7th CPC Level 2** (₹19,900 - ₹63,200; entry basic ₹19,900) per Financial Circular No. 21/2022 dated 17.08.2022.
  - During 3-year probation: Fixed minimum admissible pay of ₹19,900/month.
  - Post-probation remuneration (DA @ canonical 58% as of 2025-07-01):
    - Basic Pay: ₹19,900
    - Dearness Allowance (58%): ₹11,542
    - House Rent Allowance (8% to 16%): ₹1,592 to ₹3,184
    - Technical, field risk, and medical allowances
    - Gross Monthly Salary Estimate: ₹34,000 – ₹37,000
    - Deductions: 10% NPS/CPF (₹3,144) + Punjab Professional Tax (₹200)
    - In-Hand Monthly Salary Estimate: ₹29,500 – ₹33,000
  - Official Perks: Subsidized electricity units per PSPCL employee concession policy, comprehensive medical reimbursement, risk/hazard gear, LTC, and departmental staff quarters.
- **Career Trajectory**:
  - Assistant Lineman (ALM) — Level 2 (₹19,900 - ₹63,200)
  - → Lineman (LM) / Sub-Station Attendant (SSA) — Level 4 (₹25,500 - ₹81,100; through departmental trade test / seniority-cum-merit)
  - → Junior Engineer (JE / Electrical) / Sub-Station Officer (SSO) — Level 6 (₹35,400 - ₹1,12,400; via departmental promotion quota)
  - → Additional Assistant Engineer (AAE) / Assistant Engineer (AE / SDO) — Level 9/10 (₹47,600 - ₹1,77,500)
  - → Assistant Executive Engineer (AEE) / Executive Engineer (XEN) — Level 11 (₹67,700 - ₹2,08,700).
- **Exam Scheme (CBT)**:
  - Total duration: 3 hours (180 minutes) in a single session.
  - Objective MCQs; **no negative marking**.
  - **Part-I (Qualifying Punjabi Test)**: 50 MCQs for 50 marks. Minimum qualifying threshold: 50% (25 marks). Qualifying only; marks not counted for final merit.
  - **Part-II (Competitive Merit Paper)**: 100 MCQs for 100 marks:
    1. Technical Lineman Trade discipline questions: 50 Qs
    2. Punjabi Language Knowledge: 20 Qs
    3. General Knowledge: 10 Qs
    4. Reasoning: 10 Qs
    5. Arithmetic: 10 Qs
  - Minimum qualifying marks in Part-II: 25% for General category, 20% for Reserved categories (SC, BC, EWS, Ex-SM, FF, Sports, PwD). Final selection merit drawn strictly on normalized Part-II scores.
- **Competition Benchmarks**:
  - **2025 (CRA 312/25)**: 2,500 vacancies initially advertised; enhanced to 3,000 posts vide Corrigendum No. 01. Cutoffs: General UR 73.5, BC 70.75, EWS 68.5, SC Others 67, SC Mazhabi/Balmiki 61.25, Ex-SM Gen 47.5, Sports Gen 57.5, PwBD 51.75.
  - **2022 (CRA 299/22)**: 1,690 vacancies. Cutoffs: General UR 78, BC 74.75, EWS 72.5, SC Others 71.25, SC Mazhabi/Balmiki 64.5, Ex-SM Gen 50.25, Sports Gen 60.25, PwBD 55.
- **Audited Downloads**:

### 3.2 PSPCL Junior Engineer Exam (`pspcl-junior-engineer`)
- **File**: `public/exam-details/pspcl-junior-engineer.json`
- **Conducting Body**: Punjab State Power Corporation Limited (PSPCL), Patiala (`https://billpayment.pspcl.in`, `https://punjab.gov.in/recruitment/`)
- **Cadre**: Group 'B'/'C' Technical Supervisory Cadre (JE Electrical, JE Sub-Station, JE Civil)
- **Minimum Qualifications**:
  - Regular 3-Year Diploma in Electrical / Electrical & Electronics / Civil Engineering with minimum 60% marks (or AICTE/UGC approved B.E./B.Tech/B.Sc Engineering degree with minimum 50% marks).
  - Matriculation with Punjabi as a compulsory subject.
- **Pay Scale & Remuneration**:
  - Placed in **PSPCL Board Pay Scale / Level 6** (₹35,400 - ₹1,12,400; entry basic ₹35,400) per Financial Circular No. 21/2022.
  - During 3-year probation: Fixed minimum admissible pay of ₹35,400/month per Punjab Government Finance Department notification.
  - Post-probation remuneration (DA @ canonical 58% as of 2025-07-01):
    - Basic Pay: ₹35,400
    - Dearness Allowance (58%): ₹20,532
    - House Rent Allowance (8% to 16%): ₹2,832 to ₹5,664
    - Technical conveyance, mobile recharge, and medical allowances
    - Gross Monthly Salary Estimate: ₹60,000 – ₹65,000
    - Deductions: 10% NPS/CPF (₹5,593) + Punjab Professional Tax (₹200)
    - In-Hand Monthly Salary Estimate: ₹53,000 – ₹58,000
  - Official Perks: Free/subsidized electricity units allowance, official mobile & vehicle allowance, medical facilities for dependents, departmental quarters, LTC.
- **Career Trajectory**:
  - Junior Engineer (JE / Electrical / Sub-Station / Civil) — Level 6 (₹35,400 - ₹1,12,400)
  - → Additional Assistant Engineer (AAE) / Assistant Engineer (AE / SDO) — Level 9/10 (₹47,600 - ₹1,77,500; approx. 6–8 years service + departmental examination)
  - → Assistant Executive Engineer (AEE) / Executive Engineer (XEN) — Level 11 (₹67,700 - ₹2,08,700)
  - → Superintending Engineer (SE) — Level 12/13 (₹78,800 - ₹2,14,100)
  - → Chief Engineer (CE) / Engineer-in-Chief (EIC) — Level 14+ (₹1,44,200 - ₹2,18,200).
- **Exam Scheme (CBT)**:
  - Duration: 2 hours (120 minutes).
  - 100 Objective MCQs for 100 marks; **0.25 negative marking** (1/4th mark deduction per incorrect answer).
  - Sectional distribution:
    1. Discipline Specific Technical Syllabus (Electrical/Substation/Civil): 70 Qs (70 marks)
    2. General Knowledge / Current Awareness: 10 Qs (10 marks)
    3. Reasoning Ability: 10 Qs (10 marks)
    4. General English: 10 Qs (10 marks)
  - Minimum qualifying marks: 45% for General category, 35% for Reserved categories. Selection based strictly on merit scored in CBT followed by document verification.
- **Competition Benchmarks**:
  - **2026 (CRA 316/26)**: 622 vacancies notified for Junior Engineer (Electrical) and allied engineering positions.
  - **2024 (CRA 303/24)**: 544 vacancies (JE Electrical: 433, JE Sub-Station: 86, JE Civil: 25). Cutoff marks: General UR 71.25, BC 67.5, EWS 66.25, SC 59.75, Ex-SM Gen 48.5.
  - **2021 (CRA 298/21)**: 549 vacancies (JE Electrical: 496, JE Civil: 53). Cutoff marks: General UR 73, BC 68.75, EWS 67.5, SC 61.25, Ex-SM Gen 51.
