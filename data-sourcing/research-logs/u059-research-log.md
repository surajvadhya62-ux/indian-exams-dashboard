## 1. Summary of Unit Execution

Unit 59 encompasses the premier graduate recruitment, secondary school teacher certification, and law enforcement excise recruitments across the State of Jharkhand:
1. `jssc-cgl`: Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission Combined Graduate Level (JGGLCCE) — **Tier A (Job)**
2. `jtet`: Jharkhand Teacher Eligibility Test (JTET) — **Tier A (Entrance)**
3. `jssc-excise-constable`: JSSC Jharkhand Excise Constable Competitive Examination (JECCE) — **Tier B (Job)**

Primary research was conducted directly across Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission's official digital portal (`jssc.jharkhand.gov.in`) and Jharkhand Academic Council's statutory portal (`jac.jharkhand.gov.in`). Every populated fact has been verified against direct statutory notifications, selection rules, official result declarations, and statistical releases:

- **Recruitment Framework & Cadre Structure**:
  - `jssc-cgl` (JGGLCCE): Recruits officers into the elite Jharkhand Secretariat Service (झारखण्ड सचिवालय सेवा) as Assistant Branch Officers (सहायक प्रशाखा पदाधिकारी / ASO, Level 7), alongside key field administrative and welfare cadres including Block Supply Officers (प्रखण्ड आपूर्ति पदाधिकारी, Level 6), Block Welfare Officers (प्रखण्ड कल्याण पदाधिकारी, Level 6), Circle Inspector-cum-Kanungo (अंचल निरीक्षक-सह-कानूनगो, Level 6), Labour Enforcement Officers (श्रम प्रवर्तन पदाधिकारी, Level 6), Planning Assistants (Level 5), and Junior Secretariat Assistants (JSA, Level 2).
  - `jtet`: Statutory qualifying eligibility examination conducted by Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC), Ranchi under the Jharkhand Teacher Eligibility Test Rules 2024 (*Jharkhand Shikshak Patrata Pariksha Niyamavali, 2024*, Notification No. 487 dated 26.03.2024) and NCTE guidelines, conferring lifetime eligibility certification for appointment as Sahayak Acharya (Assistant Teachers) across Jharkhand government elementary schools (Classes 1–5 and 6–8).
  - `jssc-excise-constable` (JECCE): Recruits enforcement personnel into the Jharkhand Excise Constable Service Cadre (झारखण्ड उत्पाद सिपाही सेवा संवर्ग) under the Department of Excise and Prohibition, Government of Jharkhand (Level 2).
- **Pay Scales & Remuneration Architecture**:
  - Posts strictly mapped to the Jharkhand State Revised Pay Rules, 2017:
    - Assistant Branch Officer / Prashakha Padadhikari (ASO): Level 7 (PB-2 ₹9,300–₹34,800, GP ₹4,600, Entry basic pay: ₹44,900).
    - Block Supply Officer / Block Welfare Officer / Circle Inspector / LEO: Level 6 (PB-2 ₹9,300–₹34,800, GP ₹4,200, Entry basic pay: ₹35,400).
    - Planning Assistant: Level 5 (PB-1 ₹5,200–₹20,200, GP ₹2,800, Entry basic pay: ₹29,200).
    - Excise Constable / Junior Secretariat Assistant: Level 2 (PB-1 ₹5,200–₹20,200, GP ₹1,900, Entry basic pay: ₹19,900).
  - Dearness Allowance standardized at **58%** as of `2025-07-01` (`da_percent_as_of_review: 58`, `da_as_of: "2025-07-01"`) applied uniformly as mandated by the Project Execution Plan.
- **Examination Scheme & Selection Methodology**:
  - `jssc-cgl`: Single Mains stage (मुख्य परीक्षा) comprising 3 compulsory OMR papers held in 3 shifts of 120 minutes each on a single day:
    - Paper 1: Language Knowledge (Hindi 60 Qs + English 60 Qs = 120 Qs, 360 marks). Qualifying only (30% threshold = 108 marks). Marks do not count for merit ranking.
    - Paper 2: Selected Tribal & Regional Language (100 Qs, 300 marks). Counts towards final merit list.
    - Paper 3: General Knowledge (150 Qs, 450 marks: GS 30 Qs, Science 20 Qs, Math 20 Qs, Reasoning 20 Qs, Computer 20 Qs, Jharkhand GK 40 Qs). Counts towards final merit list.
    - Marking scheme: +3 marks per correct answer, -1 mark penalty per wrong answer. Final merit ranking derived out of 750 marks (Paper 2 + Paper 3).
  - `jtet`: Two distinct levels: Level 1 (Classes 1–5, Primary) and Level 2 (Classes 6–8, Upper Primary). Each level comprises a single composite OMR paper of 150 questions (150 marks) of 150 minutes duration (2 hours 30 minutes). No negative marking.
    - Level 1: Child Development & Pedagogy (30 Qs), Language I (30 Qs), Language II Regional/Tribal (30 Qs), Mathematics (30 Qs), Environmental Studies (30 Qs).
    - Level 2: Child Development & Pedagogy (30 Qs), Language I (30 Qs), Language II Regional/Tribal (30 Qs), Stream Subject (Math & Science, Social Studies, or Language Teacher: 50 Qs domain + 10 Qs General Computer Knowledge = 60 Qs).
    - Qualifying marks: General/EWS 60% (90 marks), BC-1/BC-2 55% (82.5 marks), SC/ST/PVTG/PwD 52% (78 marks). Lifetime certificate validity.
  - `jssc-excise-constable`: 3-stage recruitment:
    - Stage 1: Physical Efficiency Test (PET) — Male: 10 km run in 60 mins; Female: 5 km run in 40 mins. Qualifying only.
    - Stage 2: Written Examination — 3 OMR papers (+3/-1 marking): Paper 1 Language (120 Qs, qualifying 30%), Paper 2 Regional Language (100 Qs, 300 marks), Paper 3 General Knowledge (120 Qs, 360 marks). Merit out of 660 marks (Paper 2 + Paper 3).
    - Stage 3: Medical Examination by District Medical Board.
- **Competition Benchmarks**:
  - `jssc-cgl`: 2,025 vacancies in JGGLCCE-2023 (2,017 Regular under Advt. 10/2023 + 8 Backlog under Advt. 11/2023). ~6.4 lakh registered applicants; 3,04,769 appeared in September 2024; 2,145 candidates shortlisted for document verification (~1 in 150 selectivity for appeared candidates).
  - `jtet`: 26,001 vacancies notified in Sahayak Acharya recruitment for JTET qualifiers; ~3.5 lakh candidates applied for the JTET 2024/2026 cycle. (Prior benchmarks: 2016 JTET ~2.5 lakh appeared, ~53,000 qualified [21.2%]; 2013 JTET ~1.5 lakh appeared, ~52,000 qualified [34.7%]).
  - `jssc-excise-constable`: 583 vacancies (Advt. No. 06/2023); ~5.1 lakh candidates registered for PET endurance trials (~1 in 875 selectivity).
