### Statutory Context & Corporate Architecture:
- **MPPGCL Corporate Profile**:
  - MPPGCL is the successor generation company of the erstwhile Madhya Pradesh State Electricity Board (MPSEB), incorporated under the Companies Act pursuant to the MP Electricity Reforms Act, 2000.
  - Headquartered at Shakti Bhawan, Rampur, Jabalpur (482008).
  - Operates major thermal power generating stations across the state:
    - Sanjay Gandhi Thermal Power Station (SGTPS), Birsinghpur (1,340 MW)
    - Satpura Thermal Power Station (STPS), Sarni (1,330 MW)
    - Amarkantak Thermal Power Station (ATPS), Chachai (210 MW)
    - Shri Singaji Super Thermal Power Project (SSTPP), Khandwa (2,520 MW)
    - Major hydel power stations across the Narmada and surrounding river basins.
- **Pay Architecture & Statutory Standards**:

  - **Advance Increments as Personal Pay**: Vide Clause 11 of the MPPGCL Recruitment Regulations (and Advt. No. 4811/2025-26), upon successful completion of the 1-year training period and absorption into regular service, selected Junior Engineers receive **two advance increments** (2 वेतन वृद्धि) as personal pay for operational expertise.
