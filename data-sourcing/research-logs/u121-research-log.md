## 1. Administrative Framework & Statutory Authority

Recruitment to subordinate police and civil ministerial cadres across the State of Punjab is conducted through two principal statutory bodies:

### A. Punjab Police Recruitment Board / Punjab Police
- **Headquarters**: Punjab Police Headquarters, Sector 9, Chandigarh – 160 009
- **Official Domain**: `https://punjabpolice.gov.in` (and dedicated recruitment portal hubs)
- **Statutory Authority**:
  1. **The Punjab Police Act, 2007 (Punjab Act No. 10 of 2008)**: Enacted by the Punjab State Legislature to provide for the regulation, recruitment, and administration of the state police service.
  2. **The Punjab Police Rules, 1934 (as adapted and amended for Punjab)**: Regulates police organization, lower/intermediate/upper school courses for cadre advancement, appointments, discipline, and operational conditions.
  3. **The Punjab Police (Subordinate Ranks) Service Rules**: Covers recruitment, physical standards, and selection testing for Constables and Sub-Inspectors across the District Police Cadre, Armed Police Cadre, and Investigation Cadre.

### B. Punjab Subordinate Services Selection Board (PSSSB)
- **Headquarters**: Forest Complex, Sector 68, SAS Nagar (Mohali), Punjab – 160 062
- **Official Domain**: `https://sssb.punjab.gov.in`
- **Statutory Authority**:
  1. Constituted under the proviso to Article 309 of the Constitution of India for direct recruitment to all Group 'C' civil, revenue, ministerial, and technical subordinate posts under the Government of Punjab.
  2. **Punjab Civil Services (General and Common Conditions of Service) Rules, 1994 (as amended)**.
  3. **Punjab Government (Department of Personnel) Notification No. G.S.R. 56/Const./Art.309/Amd.(1)/2022 dated 28.10.2022**: Mandates a compulsory qualifying paper of Punjabi language (equivalent to PSEB Matriculation standard, minimum 50% passing threshold) for all Group 'C' recruitments in Punjab.

### C. State Pay Structure & Policy Framework (§EXECUTION-PLAN 5.3)
- **State Pay Revision Framework**: Employees of the Government of Punjab are compensated under the **Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 2021** notified by the Department of Finance, Government of Punjab (effective 01.01.2016). State pay matrices differ from central 7th CPC structures.
- **Punjab Probation Pay Policy**: Under Government of Punjab Department of Finance policy notifications (Letters No. 7/204/2012-4FP1/60 dated 15.01.2015 and No. 7/42/2020-5FP1/741-746 dated 17.07.2020), newly recruited personnel receive fixed minimum basic pay without allowances during their initial 3-year probation period. Upon completion of probation, regular allowances (DA, HRA, medical, kit, conveyance) are drawn in full.
- **DA Constant**: Standard project canonical figure of **58%** as of `2025-07-01` applied across all 4 dossiers.
