## 1. Administrative Framework & Statutory Authority

Direct recruitment to executive, armed, and technical subordinate posts in the Police, Special Protection Force (SPF), Fire and Emergency Services, and Prisons and Correctional Services Departments of the Government of Telangana is conducted by the **Telangana State Level Police Recruitment Board (TSLPRB / TGPRB)**:

### A. Telangana State Level Police Recruitment Board (TSLPRB / TGPRB)
- **Headquarters**: DGP Office Complex, Lakdi-ka-pul, Hyderabad – 500 004, Telangana
- **Official Domains & Document Repositories**:
  - Official Recruitment Portal: `https://www.tgprb.in` (and legacy domain `https://www.tslprb.in`)
  - Primary Official Document Repository: `https://doc.tgprb.in` (Amazon S3 / CloudFront backed CDN)
- **Statutory Authority & Framework**:
  1. **The Telangana Police Act and Police (Subordinate Service) Rules**: Regulates police establishment, qualifications, appointments, physical standards, discipline, and cadre hierarchy across District Police (Civil Cadre), Armed Reserve (AR), and Telangana State Special Police (TSSP / TGSP).
  2. **The Telangana Public Employment (Organization of Local Cadres and Regulation of Direct Recruitment) Order, 2018 (Presidential Order)** read with G.O. Ms No. 170 (Civil PCs), G.O. Ms No. 173 (AR PCs), and G.O. Ms No. 46, General Administration (SPF.II) Department dated 04-04-2022: Mandates 95% reservation in direct recruitment for local candidates of the respective Police Districts / Commissionerates (for Constables) and Police Zones / Multi-Zones (for Sub-Inspectors).
  3. **Government of Telangana Age Relaxation Notifications**:
     - **G.O. Ms No. 87**, General Administration (Services-A) Department dated 18-05-2026: Raised the statutory upper age limit for uniformed services by 5 (five) years.
     - **G.O. Ms No. 122**, General Administration (Services-A) Department dated 14-08-2026: Granted an additional 2 (two) years relaxation (total 7 years general relaxation above statutory limits).

### B. State Pay Structure & Policy Framework (§EXECUTION-PLAN 5.3)
- **Telangana Revised Pay Scales 2020 (RPS 2020)**: State police personnel and civil servants in Telangana are compensated strictly under the Telangana Revised Pay Scales 2020 (RPS 2020), notified vide **G.O.Ms.No.51, Finance (HRM.IV) Department, dated 11/06/2021** (effective from 01.07.2018 with monetary benefit from 01.04.2020). Central 7th CPC scales are NOT applicable to Telangana State police subordinate cadres.
- **Pay Scales**:
  - **Police Constable**: Scale 6 (₹24,280 – ₹72,850), entry basic pay ₹24,280.
  - **Sub-Inspector of Police (Civil, AR, TGSP, SPF)**: Scale 17 (₹42,300 – ₹1,15,270), entry basic pay ₹42,300.
  - *(Note: Station Fire Officers and Deputy Jailors are placed in Scale 15: ₹38,890 – ₹1,12,510).*
- **House Rent Allowance (HRA)**: Governed by **G.O.Ms.No.53, Finance (HRM.IV) Department**:
  - **24%** of basic pay for Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC) area (Hyderabad, Cyberabad, and Rachakonda Commissionerates).
  - **13%** for District Headquarters and major Municipal Corporations.
  - **11%** for other police stations and mofussil postings.
- **Dearness Allowance (DA)**: Project standard canonical figure of **58%** as of `2025-07-01` applied across both dossiers.

  1. **Stage I: Preliminary Written Test (PWT)**: 200 Objective Questions, 200 marks, 3 hours duration. Negative marking: 20% (1/5th) for wrong answers. Minimum qualifying marks: 30% for OC/EWS (60/200), 25% for BC (50/200), 20% for SC/ST/Ex-SM (40/200). Screening only.
  2. **Stage II: PMT & PET**:
     - 1600m Run (Men, max 7m 15s; Ex-SM 9m 30s) / 800m Run (Women, max 5m 20s). Qualifying for Civil, Fire, and Warder posts; graded up to 100 marks for AR, SAR CPL, and SPF.

     - Field Events (Qualifying): Long Jump (Men 4.0m, Women 2.5m) and Shot Put (7.26kg Men 6.0m / 4kg Women 4.0m).
  3. **Stage III: Final Written Examination (FWE)**: 200 Objective Questions, 3 hours duration. English, Arithmetic, General Science, History of India & Culture, Geography, Polity & Economy, Current Events, Reasoning, Personality Test (Ethics/Gender sensitivity/Emotional Intelligence), and Telangana specific contents.
     - For Civil, Fire, Warder: FWE is 200 marks (100% of final merit).
     - For AR, SAR CPL, SPF: FWE is 100 marks + PET normalized Run score 100 marks = 200 marks total merit.
- **Financial Package**: Telangana RPS 2020 Scale 6 (₹24,280 – 72,850). Entry basic pay ₹24,280. Gross estimate: ₹42,500 – ₹48,500. In-hand estimate: ₹37,500 – ₹43,000.

  - Post Code 14: SCT Reserve Sub-Inspector of Police (TGSP / TSSP) (Men)
  - Post Code 16: SCT Sub-Inspector in Telangana Special Protection Force (SPF)
  - Post Code 17: Station Fire Officer in Fire Services Department
  - Post Code 18: Deputy Jailor (Men) in Prisons Department
  - Post Code 19: Deputy Jailor (Women) in Prisons Department
- **Educational Qualification**: Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized University.
- **Selection Scheme Architecture**:
  1. **Stage I: Preliminary Written Test (PWT)**: 200 Objective Questions, 200 marks, 3 hours duration. Part A Arithmetic & Reasoning (100 Qs) + Part B General Studies & Telangana Movement (100 Qs). Negative marking: 20% (1/5th). Minimum qualifying marks: 30% for OC/EWS, 25% for BC, 20% for SC/ST/Ex-SM.
  2. **Stage II: PMT & PET**: 1600m Run (Men, max 7m 15s) / 800m Run (Women, max 5m 20s); Height 167.6 cm (Men) / 152.5 cm (Women); Long Jump 4.0m (Men) / 2.5m (Women); Shot Put 6.0m (Men) / 4.0m (Women).
  3. **Stage III: Final Written Examination (FWE) (4 Papers, 3 Hours each)**:
     - **Paper I**: English (100 marks: Part A Objective 25 marks, Part B Descriptive 75 marks). Qualifying only.
     - **Paper II**: Telugu / Urdu (100 marks: Part A Objective 25 marks, Part B Descriptive 75 marks). Qualifying only.
     - **Paper III**: Arithmetic & Test of Reasoning (Objective, 200 questions). 200 marks for Civil/Fire/Jailor; 100 marks for AR/TGSP/SPF.
     - **Paper IV**: General Studies & Telangana Movement (Objective, 200 questions). 200 marks for Civil/Fire/Jailor; 100 marks for AR/TGSP/SPF.
     - **Merit Drawing**:
       - Civil SI, SFO, Deputy Jailor: Paper III (200) + Paper IV (200) = **400 marks total merit**.
       - AR, SAR CPL, TGSP, SPF: Paper III (100) + Paper IV (100) + PET Run (100) = **300 marks total merit**.
- **Financial Package**: Telangana RPS 2020 Scale 17 (₹42,300 – 1,15,270). Entry basic pay ₹42,300. Gross estimate: ₹74,000 – ₹82,500. In-hand estimate: ₹65,500 – ₹74,000.
- **Competition Benchmarks**:
  - 2026 Cycle: 275 vacancies across 7 Police Zones (verified from Rc No. 225/Rect./Rect.-1/2026); ~2,10,000 applicants; selectivity ~1 in 764.
  - 2022 Cycle: 554 vacancies; 2,47,217 applicants; 1,05,603 qualified PWT.
